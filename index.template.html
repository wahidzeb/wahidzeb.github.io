Moto Mayhem

Moto Mayhem is a road-rash style motorbike racing game for phones and desktop browsers. Every race puts you on a winding highway against seven rival bikers, and the quickest way past them is often a well-timed punch or kick.

How to play: Throttle is automatic. Steer to stay on the road and dodge traffic and roadside objects. Ride alongside a rival and attack toward their side. Kicks reach further and hit harder but are slower than punches.

Sky Glide

Sky Glide is a one-touch flying game with a sketchbook look. You pilot a paper plane: hold to lift, release to glide, and thread your way past pencils, birds, cranes, storm clouds and even black holes.

How to play: Hold anywhere on the screen to lift the plane; release to glide down. Avoid obstacles. Passing close to one without touching it gives a near-miss bonus, and near misses in a row build a combo multiplier up to x8.

Horde Breaker

Horde Breaker is a twin-stick style zombie shooter built for touch screens. Auto-fire is on by default: you focus on moving and dodging while your gun locks onto the most dangerous zombie.

How to play: Move with the left joystick (or WASD). Your weapon aims and fires automatically. Keep moving. Brutes telegraph a charge, spitters fire acid, bloaters explode and crawlers lunge.

Goal Frenzy

Goal Frenzy is an arcade football game with a 3D-perspective stadium, curling shots and over-the-top power shots. Matches are short and fast, and they play well with a thumb on a phone or on a keyboard.

How to play: Move with the floating joystick on the left side of the screen. Tap SHOOT to shoot; hold it for more power and swipe across the button to curl the ball.

Shuttle Smash

Shuttle Smash is a badminton game for phones with a portrait 3D-perspective court and realistic shuttlecock physics, including drag and tumbling feathers. Your player moves to the shuttle automatically, so you only need to think about where and how hard to hit.

How to play: Swipe to hit: the swipe direction sets your aim and its length and speed set the power. A short swipe plays a drop or net shot, a long swipe plays a clear or lob, and a long swipe on a high shuttle is a jump smash. Tap for an automatic shot.

Kedimz Adventure

Kedimz Adventure is a mobile-first side-scrolling platformer. Kedimz the cat sets out to bring back the Golden Bells across five worlds of four levels each, and every world ends with a boss fight.

How to play: Move left and right. Keep holding a direction and Kedimz speeds up into a run. Press JUMP to jump; hold it for a higher jump. Stomp on enemies or swipe them with your paw.

Fin Frenzy

Fin Frenzy is an underwater arcade game where you play a hungry shark. Start small, eat fish to grow, and dive through five ocean zones from a sunny coral reef to the glowing darkness of the abyss, with a boss at the end of each zone.

How to play: Drag anywhere to steer. Swim into smaller fish to eat them and keep your hunger bar full; avoid bigger predators until you have grown. Tap BOOST for speed and BITE for a strong bite, and chain meals into a Frenzy.

Bus Bonanza

Bus Bonanza is a colorful parking puzzle. Tap buses to drive them out of a crowded lot to the station, where passengers in line board the bus that matches their color. It has 120 levels, a Daily Challenge, and new twists along the way: mystery buses, locks and keys, ice, garages, VIPs and timed levels.

How to play: Tap a bus that has a clear road ahead (buses only drive forward). Fill buses with matching passengers so they depart and free their bay. If every bay is full and nobody can board, you lose. Boosters such as Extra Bay, Undo, Shuffle, Magnet and Helicopter help when you are stuck.

Kedim Therapist

Kedim Therapist is a cozy comedy game about a ginger cat therapist with a Ph.D. in Napping. Silly animal patients come in with funny problems: a dog afraid of the mailman, a ghost afraid of the dark, a dragon with a temper. Help all 12 patients graduate across 36 story sessions.

Block Glide

Block Glide is a cozy color block sliding puzzle. Drag chunky clay blocks around a crowded board and slide each one out through a gate of its own color. There are 160 solver-checked levels in 8 chapters and a Daily Challenge. Along the way you meet ice, locks and keys, layered blocks, rotating blocks, shifting gates and bombs.

How to play: Drag a block to slide it. Blocks can't pass through each other, so clear a path first. A block leaves when it touches a gate of the same color and fits the gate's width. Clear the board before the timer or move counter runs out. Hammer, Freeze, Vacuum, Undo and Extra boosters help when you are stuck.

Horizon Pilot

Horizon Pilot is a 3D flight game for phones and desktop browsers. Fly 7 aircraft, from a light trainer and a vintage biplane to a floatplane, an airliner and a fighter jet, over an island with mountains, cities and 7 airports. The career has 25 missions, plus Free Flight and a Daily Challenge.

How to play: Push the throttle up and the plane takes off. Drag the stick to bank and climb; let go and assisted controls level the wings. Fly through rings, drop supplies, scoop water to fight fires, rescue sailors, do loops and rolls, then follow the glide path to land. Earn coins to upgrade and repaint your planes.

Haulway Heroes

Haulway Heroes is a 3D truck-driving career game for phones and desktop browsers. Haul general goods, fragile glass, livestock, fuel, logs, cars and oversized loads with an escort car. Routes cross highways, cities with traffic lights, mountain hairpins, deserts, snowy passes and rainy nights, and there are 6 trucks to unlock, including a hand-painted art truck.

How to play: Steer with the on-screen wheel, by tilting your phone, or with arrow buttons, and use the GAS and BRAKE pedals. Follow the GPS. Slow down for tolls, weigh stations and speed cameras, refuel at gas stations, then switch to R and back the trailer into the loading bay. You are rated and paid on time, damage and driving.

Chai Tycoon

Chai Tycoon is an idle tycoon game with a South Asian street-food feel. You start with one roadside chai stall and grow it into an empire: paratha and samosa counters, a lassi bar, a highway dhaba, cafés in a dozen cities, a delivery fleet and a chai export company. Customers walk up for steaming kulhads while the chaiwala does the long pour.

How to play: Tap the street to serve chai and start your shops. Buy more shops, hire managers so they run on their own (even offline), buy upgrades, and catch events like the Cricket Match Rush and Eid Special. Franchise to reset for Stars that boost profits for good.
