About Games by Zaib

Games by Zaib is a small independent collection of free arcade games that you can play instantly in your web browser, made with phones in mind.

About the studio

Games by Zaib is an independent studio making free, colourful browser games. The focus is on quick, fun games that are easy to pick up on a phone, with enough depth (levels, upgrades and unlocks) to keep you coming back.

How the games work

Each game is a single web page written in HTML5 and JavaScript. There is nothing to download or install and no account to create. The games work on phones, tablets and computers, with touch, keyboard and mouse controls. Music and sound effects are generated in the browser, and your progress is stored locally on your device.

The characters, art, music and code in these games are original work. Teams, clubs and rivals that appear in the sports games are fictional.

The games

  • Moto Mayhem: A motorbike combat racer: ride fast, hit hard, finish first.
  • Sky Glide: A hand-drawn paper plane flying game.
  • Horde Breaker: A top-down zombie survival shooter.
  • Goal Frenzy: Fast, glossy arcade football (soccer).
  • Shuttle Smash: 3D badminton with swipe-to-hit controls.
  • Kedimz Adventure: A side-scrolling platformer starring Kedimz the cat.
  • Meowstronauts: Two space cats fly their rockets across the galaxy. Play solo or 2-player co-op.
  • Fin Frenzy: Be the shark: eat fish, grow bigger and battle ocean bosses.
  • Bus Bonanza: Clear the jammed parking lot and send every passenger home on the right bus.

New games are added regularly. Check the home page for the latest.

Free to play
