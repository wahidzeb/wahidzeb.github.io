About Games by Zaib

Games by Zaib is a home for free, colourful games that you can play straight away on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install, no account to make and nothing to buy.

What you will find here

The site has 46 games right now, and they cover a lot of ground. Some are quick: a one-tap runner, a fruit-merging puzzle or a five-minute football match. Others are longer projects that you return to over days, like building a town from a single lemonade stand, raising a baby cat, running a farm or exploring a 3D city with a 22-mission story. Every game saves your progress on your device, so you can close it and carry on later.

Alongside the games there are guides for each one and a Tips & News section with beginner walkthroughs, round-ups and notes on what has changed. The guides explain the controls, the worlds and levels, and the tricks that help when you get stuck.

Who the games are for

Most of the games are made for everyone: kids, parents, grandparents and anyone who wants a short break. A few, such as Silly Sprout Garden, Beat Buddies, Betta Bestie and Number Dash, are built especially for younger players, with big buttons and little or no reading needed. The action games keep things cartoon-style: robots pop into bolts and stars, pirates wave a white flag and sail away, and the zombies in Horde Breaker and 30 Floors Up are goofy rather than scary.

Made by Zaib

Every game on this site is designed, built and hand-tested by Zaib. That includes the ideas, the levels, the characters, the art, the music and the writing. Zaib plays each game on phone, tablet and computer screens, in portrait and landscape, from small phones up to wide monitors, and keeps playing it after launch to fix problems and add improvements. The guides and posts on this site are written by Zaib too, from playing the games.

Characters such as Kedim the ginger cat (star of Kedim Therapist, Kedim's Snack Swing and Kedimz Adventure) and Baby Kedim the black kitten are original. Teams, clubs, rivals and towns in the games are made up.

How a game is made and tested

Each game starts with a simple idea that should be fun within the first minute, such as "tap a car to drive it out of a packed car park" or "drag a hungry black hole around a city". From there it grows in small steps: first the core action, then levels or upgrades, then sound, music and polish.

Before a game goes live, and again after every update, it is checked for things like:

  • buttons that are big enough for small fingers and never hidden behind other things on screen
  • text that fits on small phones, large tablets and wide computer screens, in both portrait and landscape
  • progress that saves properly and survives closing and reopening the game
  • a fair difficulty curve, with every level possible to finish
  • no crashes or frozen screens during long play sessions

Games are updated often. Recent examples include Boss Town Tycoon getting daily gifts, town missions and clearer number displays, Rail Rush growing to 40 levels with a Bonus Lines world, and many games getting a Share button placed so it never covers the action. You can read about changes like these in Tips & News.

Safe for kids

  • No chat, no friend lists and no way to talk to strangers.
  • No accounts, so children never type in a name, email or password.
  • No real-money purchases. Coins and upgrades are earned only by playing.
  • No gore and no bad language. Conflict is cartoon-style and silly.
  • No ads during play. If ads appear on this site, they appear only on pages such as the home page, guides and news, never inside or over a game.

Grown-ups can read the Privacy Policy to see exactly what is and is not collected.

Free to play

All the games are free. To help pay for making new games and keeping the site running, the site may show ads from Google on its pages, as explained in the Privacy Policy. The games themselves stay ad-free.

Getting help

Stuck on a level or wondering how saving works? Every game has a full guide, and you can reach Zaib with the Contact us button on the contact page.
