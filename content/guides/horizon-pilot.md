Horizon Pilot is the most "real" game on Games by Zaib. It has a full 3D island with mountains, lakes, a coastline, cities and **7 airports**, and skies that change from day to dusk, night and storms. The planes have flaps, landing gear and brakes. But the controls are assisted, so a child can take off on the first try, while grown-ups can still be challenged by a crosswind landing.

It works with touch on a phone and with a keyboard on a computer.

## Our first flight

The title screen shows your rank (we were a **Cadet, 0 of 300 XP**) and 250 coins. We chose **Career**, then the first mission of Flight School, **First Flight**. The briefing says: *"Welcome, cadet! Throttle up, lift off from Zaib International and fly through the three practice rings."* The objectives were take off, fly through 3 rings, and a **par time of 2:00**.

We pressed TAKE OFF, pushed the throttle to **100%** and rolled down the runway. We pulled back gently on the stick, and by 56 knots the little **Sparrow** was off the ground. A tip said **"You are flying! Keep climbing gently"**, and once we reached the target height the objective changed to **"Fly through the rings 0/3"**, with **+100** points for the take-off.

Then we let go of the stick, and this is where the assisted controls really help: **the plane levelled itself** and flew straight at about 780 feet. A green diamond marker showed **Ring 1** ahead, and the distance counted down: 1.7 nm, 1.4 nm, 1.2 nm, 1.0 nm. By then the clock was at 1:50, so we were never going to beat the 2:00 par on that run.

The lessons: **don't spend too long on the runway and the climb**, and **steer early**. The tip on screen says it best: "drag the stick LEFT/RIGHT to bank toward the green ring."

## Controls

| Action | Phone | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Bank, climb, dive | Stick on the left (or tilt the phone) | Arrows or WASD |
| Throttle | Slider on the right | Shift/Ctrl, R/F, or 1 to 0 (0 is full) |
| Gear and flaps | GEAR and FLAPS buttons | G and V |
| Brake | Hold BRAKE | B |
| Rudder | Automatic in assisted mode | Q and E |
| Drop cargo, water or smoke | Action button | Space |
| Camera | CAM | C |
| Pause | II | P or Esc |

**Let go of the stick and the wings level by themselves.** Hold the stick fully to one side for a roll, or fully up or down for a loop, once you are flying fast enough.

## How to take off

1. Throttle to **full**.
2. Roll straight down the runway.
3. When you reach take-off speed, **pull back gently**. Don't yank it.
4. Keep climbing gently until the objective changes.

## How to land

Landing is the hardest skill in the game, and several missions are all about it.

1. **Line up** with the runway from far away. Small corrections are easier than big ones.
2. Lower the **GEAR** and **FLAPS**.
3. **Reduce the throttle** to slow down.
4. Follow the **glide path dots** down to the runway. Airports also have PAPI lights beside the runway that help you judge your height.
5. Touch down gently, then **hold BRAKE** until you stop.

If you come in too fast or too steep, it is better to throttle up, climb away and try again.

## How stars are earned

Each mission gives a score, and the stars depend on how close you get to the best possible score: **1 star for completing it, 2 stars at 60% of the maximum, 3 stars at 82%**. Two things cap a mission at **2 stars**:

- using **AUTO** (the autopilot), and
- restarting from a checkpoint.

So AUTO is great for having a rest or learning a route, but fly it yourself to get 3 stars.

## The 25 career missions

The career is split into six chapters:

| Chapter | Plane | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Flight School | Sparrow | First Flight, Pattern Work, Final Approach, Coastal Rings, Crosswind Landing |
| Bush and Islands | Sparrow, Bumblebee, Pelican | Island Hopper, Splashdown, Mountain Supply, Engine Out!, Firefighter, Rescue at Sea |
| Airshow | Hornet | Show Off, Mesa Slalom, Low Pass, Airshow Finale |
| Business Class | Falcon | Executive Express, Storm Front, Night Arrival, Gear Jam |
| Airline Captain | Atlas | Maiden Voyage, Heavy Weather |
| Jet Ace | Viper | Afterburner, Canyon Strike, Ace Trial |

The final mission, **Ace Trial**, puts everything together: take off, run the rings, pull a loop for the crowd and land back at Zaib International.

Bigger planes also need a higher rank. Ranks go from **Cadet** through Student Pilot (300 XP), Private Pilot, Commercial Pilot, First Officer, Captain and Senior Captain, up to **Sky Legend** at 15,000 XP. Coins also buy engine upgrades, paint liveries (from Ocean Blue at 800) and smoke colours.

## Free Flight and the Daily Challenge

**Free Flight** lets you explore the whole island and hunt for **30 golden stars**. The **Daily Challenge** is a new task every day with a streak bonus (the menu showed +300 coins).

## Tips for parents

The game handles a lot of the hard parts for younger players: assisted controls, an interactive tutorial, on-screen tips and AUTO. Older children can try Engine Out! and Crosswind Landing for a real challenge. The 3D graphics need a reasonably recent phone; quality adjusts automatically.

## Questions players ask

### Do I need a joystick?
No. Touch, phone tilt and keyboard all work.

### What does AUTO do?
The autopilot flies for you, but that mission can then only earn 2 stars.

### Will my planes and mission stars stay unlocked?
Yes. Your coins, planes, upgrades, liveries, mission stars and Daily streak are kept on this device. Each mission remembers your best result.
