Kedimz Adventure will feel familiar to anyone who has played a classic platform game, but everything in it is original: the cat, the bouncy blob enemies, the music and five colourful worlds. Kedimz sets out to bring back the Golden Bells, one world at a time.

It plays well on a phone. There are big left, right, **PAW** and **JUMP** buttons at the bottom, and a keyboard works too.

## Our first level: Sunny Start

The World Map opens on **World 1, Meadow Hills** ("Sunny fields and bouncy blobs"). Only **1-1 Sunny Start** is open. Under each level are **three gem outlines**: those are the Kitty Gems hidden in it.

We pressed START and held right, jumping every second or so. That was a bit too simple: we bumped into green blobs early on and dropped to **one heart out of three**. We kept going, got back to two hearts, collected coins along a floating wooden platform, and hopped over a gap. We passed a green pipe with a little sign pointing **down** next to it, which means you can go down that pipe into a secret room. We ran past it.

At **0:26.5** we reached the end: **Level Clear!**, +13 coins, score 2,830, and **0 of 3 Kitty Gems**. The level was done, but the gems were all still hidden.

The lesson: **rushing gets you through, but exploring is how you get gems.** Look for signs, pipes and blocks above your head.

## Controls

| Action | Phone | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Move | ◀ ▶ buttons (or joystick) | Arrow keys or A, D |
| Jump | JUMP (hold to jump higher) | Space, Up, W, Z or K |
| Paw swipe, fire, ice | PAW | X, J, C or Shift |
| Go down a pipe | | Down or S |
| Pause | ⏸ | Esc or P |

**Keep holding a direction and Kedimz breaks into a run.** You can also still jump a moment after running off a ledge, which helps a lot with gaps.

In Settings you can switch to a joystick, change the button size, and use a **left-handed layout**.

## Damage is forgiving

This is a great game for younger players because mistakes are not harsh:

- If Kedimz is big, a hit makes him **small first**. Only after that do you lose a heart.
- **Falling into a pit costs a heart** and puts you back on safe ground. It does not end the level.
- **Checkpoints** refill your hearts.
- You start with 3 lives, and **every 100 coins gives an extra life**.

## How to beat enemies

**Stomp** on enemies by landing on top of them, or **paw swipe** them when they are in front of you. Blobs are the easiest. **Spiky enemies cannot be stomped or swiped**, so throw a fire or ice shot, use a Star, or simply jump over them.

## Power-ups

Hit blocks and break bricks to find:

| Power-up | What it does |
|---|---|
| Size Up | Makes Kedimz big, so a hit only shrinks him |
| Fire Paw | Throws fireballs |
| Ice Paw | Freezes enemies |
| Wings | A double jump |
| Coin Magnet | Pulls nearby coins to you |
| Star | Short invincibility |

## The five worlds and bosses

Each world has three normal levels and a boss level:

| World | What it is like | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| Meadow Hills | Sunny fields and bouncy blobs | Grubbo the Mole King |
| Dune Desert | Scorching sands and cactus pods | Scarabo the Sand Tank |
| Crystal Ice Caves | Slippery floors and falling icicles | Frostfang the Yeti |
| Lava Castle | Fire bars, crushers and molten pits | Magmor the Lava Golem |
| Sky Kingdom | Floating islands above the clouds | Tempest the Storm Owl |

In the Ice Caves the floor is slippery, so start slowing down earlier than you think. **Beat each boss to open the next world.**

## Kitty Gems and best times

There are **3 Kitty Gems in every level**, 60 in total, and the map tracks your best time for each level. Gems are often:

- in a **secret room** down a pipe (look for a sign pointing down),
- high up, reached from a spring or a moving platform,
- behind a row of bricks you can break.

A good plan is to clear a level first, then **replay it slowly** looking for gems.

## The Shop

Coins buy cat skins, hats and upgrades.

- **Skins:** Ginger (free), Midnight 150, Snowball 250, Grey Tabby 350, Calico 500, Siamese 650, Mint Pop 900, Galaxy 1,600 and Golden 2,600.
- **Hats:** from the Red Cap at 100 to the Royal Crown at 1,300 and the Halo at 2,000.
- **Upgrades:** Extra Heart (400, then 1,000), Start Big (600), Magnet Range (300, then 700), Extra Life (500, then 1,200) and Lucky Paw (+25% coins, 1,500).

**Our tip:** buy **Extra Heart** first. One more heart makes every level easier, especially boss fights. **Start Big** is the next best buy.

## Fixes since launch

Saves are now checked when they load so a damaged save cannot break the game, the shop was made more compact on phones held sideways, and a share button was added.

## Questions we hear a lot

### Is Kedimz Adventure good for young children?
Yes. There is no blood or scary content, and falling or getting hit is forgiving.

### How many levels are there?
20: five worlds of four levels each, including five boss levels.

### Do I keep my gems and hats?
Yes. Your coins, unlocked levels, collected gems, skins, hats and upgrades are kept on this device, so Kedimz can carry on from the last level you reached.

### Does it work sideways?
Yes, and the game suggests landscape for the best view.
