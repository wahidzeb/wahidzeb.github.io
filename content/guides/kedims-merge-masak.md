## What kind of game is it?

Kedim's Merge Masak is a merge-2 cooking game. Masak means "to cook" in Malay. Kedim the cat runs a festive open house stall, and guests drop by one after another, each with a bubble showing the dish they want. The board below the stall is a 6 by 7 grid where all the cooking happens.

There is no energy bar and no timer. Guests wait patiently, so you can cook at your own pace. It is a calm game for kids and grown-ups alike.

## How to play

1. **Tap a basket or pot** on the board to get a basic ingredient. Each festival has two of these generators.
2. **Drag one item onto another of the same kind** to merge them into the next dish.
3. **Serve guests.** When a dish in a guest's bubble is on your board, tap the bubble or drag the dish onto it. A SERVE label shows when you have it ready.
4. **Earn coins** for every guest. Bigger dishes pay more.
5. **Keep the board tidy.** Drag dishes you do not need onto the **Bin** to sell them for 1 coin each.

## The recipe chains

Every festival has two chains, each with five dishes. Merging two of a dish makes the next one.

| Festival | Generator | Dishes, from first to last |
|---|---|---|
| Hari Raya | Palm Leaf Basket | Palm Leaf, Woven Leaf, Ketupat, Ketupat Bunch, Ketupat & Rendang |
| Hari Raya | Kampung Oven | Flour, Cookie Dough, Tart Nenas, Cookie Jar, Raya Cookie Tray |
| Chinese New Year | Bamboo Steamer | Flour Bowl, Wrapper, Dumpling, Steamer Basket, Reunion Dumplings |
| Chinese New Year | Mandarin Tree | Blossom, Mandarin, Mandarin Pair, Mandarin Plate, Lucky Hamper |
| Deepavali | Kuali Wok | Rice Flour, Dough Coil, Murukku, Murukku Tin, Deepavali Snack Tray |
| Deepavali | Milk Pot | Milk, Laddu, Laddu Trio, Sweet Plate, Mithai Gift Box |

A guest pays 2, 8, 15, 25 or 40 coins depending on how far up the chain their dish is. The Recipe Book keeps every dish you have found, with a short fact about each one.

## Decorations

Tap **Decorate** to spend coins on the stall. Each festival has five decorations, costing 20, 40, 60, 90 and 120 coins. For Hari Raya they are Lampu Lip-Lap, a Ketupat Garland, a Raya Banner, a Mengkuang Mat and a Pelita Row. Chinese New Year has red lanterns, lucky knots, a Gong Xi banner, a red carpet and mandarin trees, and Deepavali has fairy lights, a marigold garland, a banner, kolam art and diya lamps. They appear on the stall straight away.

## Festivals and stars

Serve **15 guests** to finish a festival. You get a 50 coin bonus and the next festival opens: Hari Raya first, then Chinese New Year, then Deepavali. You can keep cooking at a finished festival whenever you like.

Each festival has 3 stars, 9 in total:

- Serve 15 guests.
- Buy all 5 decorations.
- Cook both top dishes, the last one in each chain.

## Tips

1. **Merge before you tap again.** Each tap on a generator fills a square, so merge pairs first to keep space free.
2. **Look at the bubbles.** Cook toward what guests ask for, and serve small dishes quickly to keep the queue moving.
3. **Keep one or two empty squares** so you always have room for new ingredients.
4. **Sell spares.** If you have a dish nobody wants and no partner for it, drag it to the Bin.
5. **Save up for the top dishes.** They need 16 basic ingredients each, so build them slowly while you serve smaller orders.

## Questions players ask

### Is Kedim's Merge Masak free?

Yes. All three festivals are free in the browser, with no download, no account, no energy timer and no ads during play.

### Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It is a gentle game with friendly cat guests, no timers and nothing to lose.

### Where is my progress saved?

Your board, coins, decorations and Recipe Book are saved on your device in this browser.

### Is there music?

No. The game has sound effects only, and you can turn them off in Settings.
