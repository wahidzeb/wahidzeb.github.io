## What kind of game is it?

Kedim Laundry Shop is a time management game set in a small laundry. Customers queue at the counter with a basket of clothes and a patience heart above their head. Your job is to move every basket through the machines, from washer to dryer to folding table, and hand it back before the heart runs out.

Each day has a set number of customers and a coin goal with three stars. Days are short, so it is easy to play one more. Coins you earn are always yours to keep, and the Shop turns them into faster machines and a cosier shop.

## How to play

1. **Tap a customer** at the counter. Their clothes jump into a free washer.
2. **Wait for the green tick.** When a washer is done, tap it to send the load to a dryer. When a dryer is done, tap it to send the load to the folding table.
3. **Fold.** Tap the folding table once for each piece of clothing. When the pile is folded, it goes back to its owner and you get paid.
4. **Watch the hearts.** A customer whose heart runs out leaves without paying. Once their clothes are in a machine, the heart drains much more slowly, so getting baskets started early helps.
5. **Empty the dryers.** Clothes left in a finished dryer start to steam after a few seconds, then turn wrinkly. A wrinkly load pays less and gets no tip.

Day 1 shows you every step with a pointing hand, so you can learn as you play.

## Coins, tips and combos

A small basket pays 10 coins and a medium one 14. Happy customers add a tip on top, and the fuller their heart, the bigger the tip. Serve customers close together, within a few seconds of each other, to build a **combo**: every step adds 10% more, up to 50%.

Fill the coin bar at the top for stars. One star opens the next day, and three stars is a perfect day. If a day goes badly, you still keep every coin you earned, and you can play it again.

## New twists as the days go on

| Day | Twist | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Stains | Some baskets have a mud, juice or ink stain. Tap the customer, then pick the spray of the same colour. The wrong spray makes them wait longer. Stained loads pay a little extra. |
| 5 | Lost socks | Now and then a sock pops out of a dryer. Tap it before it fades for bonus coins. |
| 7 | Express customers | Customers with a lightning bolt pay double, but their heart runs out faster. Serve them first. |
| 10 | Ironing board | Shirts go to the ironing board instead of the folding table. Tap the board to press them smooth. |
| 14 | Big baskets | Huge baskets take longer to wash, dry and fold, but pay much more. |

## Upgrades

Open the **Shop** from the title screen or after a day. Upgrades last forever.

| Upgrade | What it does | Prices |
|---|---|---|
| Extra washer | One more washing machine (you start with 2, up to 4) | 150, 450 |
| Extra dryer | One more dryer (you start with 1, up to 3) | 120, 400 |
| Second folding table | Fold two loads at once | 350 |
| Quick wash | Washers finish faster | 70, 150, 300, 550 |
| Hot dryers | Dryers finish faster | 70, 150, 300, 550 |
| Neat folding | Fewer taps to fold and iron | 100, 260, 500 |
| Comfy bench | Customers wait longer before getting upset | 90, 240, 460 |
| Snack corner | Happy customers leave bigger tips | 110, 280, 520 |

A good order for your first coins is **Quick wash** and **Hot dryers** level 1, then an **Extra dryer**, because drying is usually the slowest step. The **Comfy bench** helps a lot once express customers arrive.

## Decorations

The Decor tab has seven decorations: a Leafy plant (60 coins), Wall clock (80), Pink rug (110), Cosy lamp (140), Shiny poster (170), Fishbowl (220) and Glowing sign (300). They appear in the shop straight away, and every one you own makes customers a little more patient.

## Daily gift

Come back each day for a free gift of coins. The gift grows over a 7-day streak, from 20 coins on the first day to 150 on the seventh. Miss a day and the streak starts again from the first gift.

## Tips

1. **Keep every machine busy.** Start a new wash while the dryer is still running, so nothing sits empty.
2. **Serve the most worried customer first.** The customer with the emptiest heart should be the next one you tap.
3. **Never leave a finished dryer waiting.** Watch for the steam warning and empty it before the clothes go wrinkly.
4. **Fold in quick bursts.** Folding is all tapping, so do it between machine jobs rather than all at the end.
5. **Replay early days.** If a new day feels hard, replay an earlier one for coins, buy an upgrade and come back.

## Questions players ask

### Is Kedim Laundry Shop free?

Yes. All 30 days are free in the browser, with no download, no account and no ads during play.

### How long is a day?

Early days take about a minute or two. Later days have more customers and take a few minutes.

### Where is my progress saved?

Your days, stars, coins, upgrades and decorations are saved on your device in this browser.

### Is there music?

No. The game has light sound effects only, and you can turn them off in Settings.
