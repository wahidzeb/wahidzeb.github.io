Kedim, the ginger cat from Kedim Therapist and Kedimz Adventure, is hungry. His fish treat dangles from ropes, and your job is to cut them at the right moment so the treat swings, flies and drops into his mouth. Kedim watches the treat with his eyes, opens wider as it gets close, chomps happily when he catches it and sulks when it falls.

Our very first level took a single swipe: one rope straight above Kedim, three stars in a line below the fish. Cut, drop, "Snack time!", all 3 stars and 25 coins. Later boxes are a very different story.

## How to play

- **Swipe across a rope to cut it.** With a mouse, drag across it. One swipe can cut more than one rope, so aim carefully.
- **Time your cuts.** Let the treat swing, then cut when it is moving toward Kedim.
- **Collect the stars** on the way. They are not required to finish a level, but they open the later boxes.
- **Restart** with the circular arrow at the top, and **pause** with the button top left.

A level is done when Kedim catches the treat. You lose it if the treat falls off the screen, hits spikes or gets stolen by a spider.

## The six Snack Boxes

There are 120 levels in six boxes of 20, with 3 stars each, so 360 stars in total. Each new box needs a number of stars:

| Box | Levels | Stars needed | New idea introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kitchen Box | 1-1 to 1-20 | Open from the start | Ropes, then bubbles from level 1-9 |
| Garden Box | 2-1 to 2-20 | 30 | Air cushions, then spikes from 2-9 |
| Toy Box | 3-1 to 3-20 | 70 | Green rings that grab the treat with a new rope (from 3-9) |
| Space Box | 4-1 to 4-20 | 115 | The gravity switch, then bouncy pads from 4-9 |
| Magic Box | 5-1 to 5-20 | 165 | Portals |
| Spooky Box | 6-1 to 6-20 | 215 | Spiders |

New gadgets arrive one at a time, so each box first teaches the idea gently, then mixes it with everything you already know.

## The gadgets explained

- **Bubbles** lift the treat upward. Tap the treat to pop the bubble and let it drop.
- **Air cushions** blow the treat in the direction they point. Tap a cushion to puff.
- **Spikes** pop the treat. Swing it around them, never through them.
- **Green rings** (auto-ropes) grab the treat with a fresh rope when it passes close.
- **Gravity switch:** tap it to flip gravity, so the treat falls upward.
- **Bouncy pads** bounce the treat off at an angle.
- **Portals** teleport the treat from one to the other, but they snap any ropes still attached.
- **Spiders** crawl down a rope toward the treat. Cut that rope before the spider arrives.
- **Moving pins** carry a rope's anchor back and forth, which changes the swing.

## Tips for 3 stars

1. **Watch first.** Before touching anything, watch a full swing or two. Most levels show you the answer if you look at where the treat goes.
2. **Count the ropes.** With two or three ropes, the order you cut them matters more than the timing. Cutting the rope on one side swings the treat toward the other side.
3. **Use the arc.** The treat keeps moving the way it was swinging when you cut. Cut at the bottom of a swing to send it far, at the edge to drop it close.
4. **Pop bubbles late.** A bubble can float the treat up past a star before you pop it over Kedim.
5. **Restart freely.** Restarting costs nothing, and a level often needs two or three tries to get the timing.
6. **Every level can be solved.** If you are truly stuck, tap the light bulb for a hint. It replays the full solution. You start with 3 hints and can buy more with coins.

## Coins, the Daily Snack and the shop

Finishing levels pays coins (25 for our first 3-star level), and awards pay extra (our first one, First Bite, gave 20). The **Daily Snack** on the title screen is a special level each day worth 40 coins, with a streak bonus if you come back daily. Coins buy more hints, 9 treat skins and 11 hats for Kedim. There are 20 awards to collect.

## What changed after testing

Recent updates drew more detailed fish, twisted ropes, shiny faceted stars and metal spikes and anchors, and turned the background into a cardboard box with a spotlight on Kedim. Settings now fit on wide phone screens, and a More games link was added to the results.

## FAQ

### How many stars do I need for the Spooky Box?
215 stars. Garden needs 30, Toy 70, Space 115 and Magic 165.

### Is every level possible?
Yes. Every level can be solved, and the hint shows the full solution.

### How do I pop a bubble?
Tap the treat while it is inside the bubble.

### What do I do about spiders?
Cut the rope the spider is crawling down before it reaches the treat.

### Does it save?
Yes. Your stars, coins, skins and hats are saved on your device.
