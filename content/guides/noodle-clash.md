## Seventeen seconds

On our first Classic run we steered around grabbing food, felt pleased with ourselves, and then drove our head straight into the side of a bigger noodle called Captain Chow. **CRASHED!** Peak length 56, best rank #15, 24 orbs eaten, 17 seconds. It was a very quick lesson in the one rule that matters: **your head must never touch another noodle's body.** Their head touching your body is what you want.

## How to play

You are a noodle in a round arena full of glowing food orbs, sitting in a bowl with steam rising off it. Eat orbs to grow longer and thicker. Climb the live leaderboard in the top corner and the leader wears a crown. Any noodle whose head touches another noodle's body explodes into a pile of big, bright orbs. The red border around the arena is deadly too, and crystals and rocks block the way.

| Action | Phone | Computer |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | Drag anywhere (a joystick appears under your thumb), or choose Follow finger in Settings | Point with the mouse, or Left/Right or A/D |
| Boost | Hold the lightning button, or tap with a second finger | Hold the mouse button, Space, Up or W |
| Pause | II button | P or Esc |

The short tutorial teaches steering, eating, boosting and crashing in four steps. You can skip it.

## Boosting: fast but costly

Boosting makes you much faster, but it **burns your length** and leaves a trail of orbs behind you. Use it in short bursts for a reason:

- to cut in front of a rival so they crash into you,
- to escape when someone is about to trap you,
- to grab the big orbs from a fresh crash before anyone else.

Never boost just to get somewhere. Slow and steady keeps you long.

## How to make other noodles crash

1. **Cut across their path.** Get slightly ahead of a rival, then steer across in front of their head. If they can't turn in time, they hit your body.
2. **Coil around smaller noodles.** When you are long, circle a smaller noodle and slowly close the loop. They have nowhere to go.
3. **Feast on the orbs.** When a noodle crashes, its body turns into big glowing orbs worth far more than normal food.

## Know your rivals

The computer noodles have personalities:

| Type | What it does | How to handle it |
|---|---|---|
| Hunters | Chase heads and cut across paths | Don't run in a straight line next to them |
| Cowards | Flee from anything bigger | Easy pickings once you are larger |
| Encirclers | Coil around smaller noodles | Stay away from big ones until you are bigger, and never let one get around you |
| Grazers | Go for the richest food | They gather near crash orbs, so they are often distracted |

## The four modes

| Mode | Rules | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Classic | Endless arena. Grow huge and rule the leaderboard | Play safe early. Size is protection |
| Rush (3 minutes) | Double orbs and you respawn. Longest at the end wins | Be bolder here, since a crash is not the end |
| Battle Royale | The zone shrinks and there are no respawns. Last noodle wins | Stay inside the purple zone. Outside it you shrink quickly |
| Duo (2 players) | Same phone, split screen in landscape, 3-minute duel | Player 1 steers on the left half, Player 2 on the right |

## Beginner tips

- Stay near the middle at first, away from the deadly border and the crowded crash zones.
- Leave space around your head. Most crashes happen when you squeeze through a gap that closes.
- Turn in wide curves. Sharp, panicked turns often put your head straight into a body.
- Watch the minimap to see where the big noodles are.

## Coins, missions and the shop

Every match pays coins based on how you did (even our 17-second run earned 5). There are 3 daily missions and 19 awards that pay more. The Shop sells 18 skins, from free Chili Oil and Matcha to Rainbow Dragon and Royal Gold (2,500 coins), and 8 patterns such as Stripes, Polka Dots and the animated Neon Pulse. They only change how your noodle looks.

## What changed after testing

Updates turned the food into little noodle-bowl treats (dumplings, eggs, shrimp, bok choy and chillies) that bob and glow, gave the arena a ceramic bowl rim, ripples and steam, and made the noodle bodies richer. Numbers for coins, scores and missions now use separators and short forms so they always fit, and a More games link was added to the results screen.

## FAQ

### How do I stop crashing in Noodle Clash?
Give your head room, avoid squeezing between two noodles, and stay off the red border. Only your head touching something causes a crash.

### Does boosting make me shorter?
Yes. Boosting burns length and drops orbs behind you, so use it in short bursts.

### Can two people play?
Yes. Duo is a split-screen, 3-minute duel on one phone. Turn the phone sideways.

### Does it need the internet?
The rivals are computer-controlled noodles, so there is no waiting for other players to join.

### Does it save?
Yes. Your coins, skins, missions and awards are saved on your device.
