Baby Kedim is a little black kitten who has just moved into a bright, cosy home, and you are in charge of keeping him happy. He needs four things: **food, fun, a bath and sleep**. Coloured rings on the room buttons at the bottom of the screen show how full each need is, so you can see at a glance what he wants next.

He is a very forgiving pet. Kedim never gets hurt or sick. If you forget about him, he just gets a bit sulky or messy, says "I missed you!" when you come back, and stops growing until you look after him again.

## The six rooms

Tap the tabs along the bottom to move around the house.

| Room | What you do there | Need it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Drag a food to Kedim's mouth | Food |
| Living | Pet him for purrs, throw the ball, talk, play mini-games | Fun |
| Garden | Plant seeds, water them twice, harvest, sell at the Farm Stand, play Garden Ball | Fun, and crops to feed him |
| Hobby | Play the piano, paint a picture for the wall, write cute poems | Fun |
| Bath | Rub the soap all over him, then tap Rinse | Bath |
| Bed | Tap Lights off for a nap. He wakes up when he is rested | Sleep |

In the kitchen, the first food is free and the others cost a few coins each (between 4 and 14 when we played). Crops from the garden can also be fed to him, which saves coins.

## How growing up really works

Kedim grows in four stages: **Kitten, Kid, Teen and Adult**. The top bar shows his stage and how long until the next one ("Grows in 10m" on day one).

The important detail: **he only grows while all four of his needs are reasonably full**. If any need drops below about a third, the growth timer pauses. So the trick is not to max out one thing but to keep every ring topped up.

| Stage | Growing time needed |
|---|---|
| Kitten to Kid | 10 minutes |
| Kid to Teen | 1 day |
| Teen to Adult | 2 days |

These are growing times, not calendar times: the clock only runs while he is happy. Putting him to bed before you close the game is a good habit, because his needs fall more slowly while he sleeps.

## Choose his grown-up form

When Kedim becomes an adult, he turns into one of three grown-up cats. Each pair of mini-games counts toward one form, and the form whose games he played most wins:

| Grown-up form | Mini-games that count | What the game says |
|---|---|---|
| Therapist Kedim | Feel-Good Clinic, Yarn Pop | "A kind, wise listener" |
| Snack-Swing Kedim | Snack Swing, Fish Catch | "A happy, round snack lover" |
| Adventurer Kedim | Kedim Dash, Treasure Dive | "A brave explorer" |

Each form is a nod to another of our games: Kedim Therapist, Kedim's Snack Swing and Kedimz Adventure. If you have a favourite, play its two mini-games most often during the Kid and Teen stages.

## The six mini-games

Open them from the Mini-games button in the living room. Each one pays coins.

- **Snack Swing:** cut the rope so the treat swings into Kedim's mouth
- **Fish Catch:** slide to catch falling fish and dodge old boots
- **Feel-Good Clinic:** give each patient what they need, fast
- **Yarn Pop:** pop the floating yarn balls, and chain them for combos
- **Kedim Dash:** tap to jump over boxes and grab coins
- **Treasure Dive:** swim down, grab gems and dodge jellyfish

The garden also has its own game, **Garden Ball**: tap the ball to keep it bouncing.

## The garden

Plant seeds in the raised beds, **water each one twice**, then harvest when they are ready. You can sell crops at the Farm Stand for coins or feed them to your kitty. Every harvest helps your garden grow bigger. It is a nice way to earn coins slowly while Kedim naps.

## The hobby room

This is the creative corner. Play a tune on the piano, paint a picture that then hangs on the wall, or write a short, cute poem. There is no score here, it is just for fun, and it cheers Kedim up.

## Dressing up and decorating

The buttons on the right side open the wardrobe and decorations. Coins buy hats, collars, glasses and other outfits, plus things for the house such as rugs and pictures. The book button keeps a record of what you have collected, and the gift button gives a daily present.

## More cats

Tap the little kitty face at the top of the screen to adopt **up to three cats**. Each one has their own name, fur colour and needs, and there are ten fur coats to choose from. In Settings, the cat art option can match the fur colour you picked.

## Tips

- Start every visit with the daily gift, then check the rings and fix the lowest need first.
- Feed garden crops instead of buying food to save coins for outfits.
- Do the bath before bedtime so two needs are full while he sleeps.
- The game can offer a gentle reminder when Kedim gets hungry or misses you, at most once a day. It only appears if you say yes.

## Changes after our first weeks with Kedim

Recent updates made the home feel alive: curtains sway, a pendant lamp swings, magnets wobble on the fridge, and a beam of window light catches floating dust. Kedim was drawn bigger on upright phones, and on very short, wide screens he no longer hides under the top bar. Scores and streaks in the mini-games now use compact numbers so they always fit.

## FAQ

### Can Baby Kedim get sick or run away?
No. He only gets sulky or messy if you forget him, and he stops growing until you care for him again.

### How long does it take to grow up?
At least three days and ten minutes of growing time in total, and the timer only runs while all his needs are fairly full.

### How do I get Therapist Kedim?
Play Feel-Good Clinic and Yarn Pop more than the other mini-games while he grows up. Those two count toward the Therapist form.

### Can I have more than one cat?
Yes, up to three. Tap the kitty face at the top of the screen.

### Will Kedim remember me tomorrow?
Yes. Kedim's name, age, needs, outfits, coins, garden and hobby room are kept on this device. The game also notes when you left, so he keeps growing while you are away and may be a little hungry or sleepy when you come back.
