Critter Keep Rescue is soft and friendly from top to bottom. Your keep is a little garden of tree stumps where round, colourful critters live and earn coins. The bandits are grey raccoons in masks, and when they catch you they just giggle: "Gotcha! Hehe!", "Tag, you're it!". Then you go back to the start. Nobody gets hurt.

## Our first rescue attempt

The game starts in your **Keep** with two critters already living there: **Pipkin** (a bunny who "loves hopping on wooden pads") and **Mossy** (a blob with a sprout on its head). A help card explains everything, and then the **Rescue Lv 1** button takes you to the map.

**Level 1, Meadow Hollow** is a small hedge maze with wooden crates, flowering bushes and coins on the grass. You start on the **HOME** mat at the bottom. A raccoon bandit patrols near the middle, and in front of him is a **yellow cone**: that is what he can see.

We walked up the left side and stepped into a bush. "**Hiding in a bush! Bandits can't see you here.**" From there we spotted the critter's **cage in the top-left corner**, tucked behind a hedge. Our first try to reach it straight across failed: the hedge blocks the way, and you have to go around.

On the way back we got careless. The raccoon had wandered down towards the start, we walked straight into his cone, and: **"Caught! Back to HOME you go."** He caught us right next to the home mat.

The lessons:

1. **Watch the cone, not the raccoon.** Bandits turn, and the cone swings with them.
2. **Bushes are safe spots.** Hop from bush to bush and wait for the cone to swing away.
3. **Plan the route before you move.** Cages are often behind hedges, so look for the way round.

## Controls

- **Drag anywhere** to move (or use the arrow keys or WASD on a computer).
- Tap **DASH** for a quick burst of speed. It has a short cooldown.
- **Careful:** dashing through a bush does not hide you. You are only hidden while you are standing or walking in it.

## How stars work

Each rescue level gives up to **3 stars**, based on how many times you were caught:

| Times caught | Stars |
|---|---|
| Never | 3 stars |
| 1 or 2 | 2 stars |
| 3 or more | 1 star |

You always finish the level in the end, so younger players never get stuck. Replaying a level for 3 stars still pays coins, just less than the first clear.

## The six biomes

There are **36 levels** in six biomes of six, each worth up to 18 stars:

| Biome | Look |
|---|---|
| Meadow Hollow | Hedges and flowers |
| Whisper Woods | Trees and falling leaves, a little darker |
| Sunny Shore | Sand and water |
| Frosty Peaks | Ice and snow |
| Canyon Dunes | Red rock and dust |
| Moonlit Town | Houses at night, with fireflies |

The last level of each biome is a tougher **boss level**, which pays double coins.

## Eggs and merging

Coins from your keep and from rescues buy **eggs**:

| Egg | Cost | What hatches |
|---|---|---|
| Meadow Egg | 150 | Mostly Common, some Uncommon, rarely Rare |
| Fancy Egg | 900 | Uncommon to Epic, a tiny chance of Legendary |
| Royal Egg | 5,000 | Rare, Epic or Legendary (10% Legendary) |

**Merge** two of the same critter to get a critter of the next rarity. A little badge appears on the Merge button whenever you can merge.

There are **30 critters** across five rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Rarer critters earn far more coins in your keep. The three Legendaries are **Sunny Drake**, **Aurora Fawn** and **Cosmo**. The **Book** shows every critter you have found.

## Defending your keep

After you clear **Level 2**, bandits start sneaking into your keep to borrow critters. **Tap a bandit** to shoo it away. In **Defend** you can build:

| Defence | Levels | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Picket Fence | 5 | 250 to 10,000 | Bandits take longer to climb in |
| Alarm Bell | 3 | 400 to 5,000 | Rings early and slows sneaky paws |
| Guard Puppy | 3 | 800 to 9,000 | Chases bandits away by itself |

**Our tip:** build the first **Picket Fence** level early, then save for the **Guard Puppy**, which protects the keep while you are away.

## Quests and daily rewards

**Quests** give coins for things like "Rescue 3 critters", "Hatch 1 egg", "Merge 1 critter", "Shoo away 2 bandits", "Get 3 stars in 1 level" or "Pet critters 10 times". There is also a daily reward that grows with your streak, including free eggs.

## FAQ

### Is it scary?
No. Being caught just sends you back to the start with a giggle.

### How many levels are there?
36 rescue levels across six biomes.

### Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device.
