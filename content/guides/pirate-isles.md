Pirate Isles is drawn like a picture book: turquoise water, sandy islands with palm trees, little harbour towns with coloured roofs and a wooden sloop with a red flag. Battles are cartoon-style. Your cannons fire by themselves, and beaten pirates wave a white flag and sail off. Nobody is hurt.

## Our first voyage

We started in **Coral Bay** with **60 gold** at **Rank 1**. The first story mission, **Learn the ropes**, simply says: "Sail to the glowing buoy." We held the right arrow (with a touch of up) and the sloop picked up speed. A few seconds later we reached the buoy: **+40 gold**, and straight away a new mission, **Floating treasure**: "Collect 3 floating chests."

We kept holding right, which was a mistake. The sloop sailed past **Pebble Isle**, and the **yellow arrow** at the edge of the screen pointed back the way we came: the chests were 114 metres behind us. Since we were right next to **Palm Harbor**, we sailed into its **anchor ring** and docked. The dock menu showed **Missions, Shipyard and Repair**, and three port contracts: Shipwreck salvage (135 gold), a Pirate bounty (115) and a Treasure map for Pebble Isle (175).

The lessons:

1. **Follow the yellow arrow.** It always points to your current mission, with the distance.
2. **Let go to slow down.** The ship keeps sailing while you hold a direction.
3. **Docking at a new port is useful.** You can take contracts, repair and shop there.

## Controls

- **Drag anywhere** and the ship turns and sails towards your finger. **Drag further to sail faster.**
- On a keyboard, use **WASD or the arrow keys**.
- **Cannons fire automatically** at the nearest pirate ship in range.
- Sail **slowly** into a harbour's **anchor ring** and tap **Dock** (or press **E**).

Your ship slowly **repairs itself** when you are out of battle. If it is wrecked, you restart at the last port you docked at.

## How cargo missions work

Dock to **load the crates**, then sail to the destination and **dock again to deliver**. Your **Cargo Hold** limits how many crates you can carry. The crate counter at the top of the screen (📦 0/3 at the start) shows what you have on board. The glass delivery has a **time limit**, so upgrade your sails first.

## Fighting pirates

- **Keep moving.** Your cannons aim themselves, so steering is all about dodging cannonballs.
- **Circle, don't charge.** Sailing in circles around a pirate keeps you in range while making you hard to hit.
- **Retreat to repair.** Sail away from battle and your hull slowly heals.
- For the bosses, **upgrade Hull and Cannons** first.

## Ports and islands

**Four harbour towns:** Coral Bay (where you start), Palm Harbor, Frost Point (snowy roofs, in the north-east) and Sunset Town.

**Eight wild islands:** Turtle Isle, Skull Rock, Banana Key, Gull Island, Coconut Cay, Mango Isle, Shell Atoll and Pebble Isle. Treasure missions send you to dig on these.

## Shipyard

**Upgrades** (each up to 5 levels, costing 120, 300, 650, 1,200 and 2,200 gold):

- **Hull:** more health
- **Sails:** faster sailing
- **Cannons:** harder, faster shots and a little more range
- **Cargo Hold:** carry 2 more crates per level

**Ships:**

| Ship | Cannons | Cost | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sloop | 1 | Free | 1 |
| Brigantine | 2 | 1,500 | 3 |
| Galleon | 3 | 5,000 | 6 |

Bigger ships have much more health and also carry more cargo.

**Our order:** one level of **Sails** (deliveries get easier), then **Hull**, then save for the **Brigantine** as soon as you reach Rank 3.

## FAQ

### Is Pirate Isles violent?
No. Battles are cartoon-style and beaten pirates wave a white flag and sail away.

### What happens if my ship is wrecked?
You restart at the last port you docked at.

### Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device.
