## What kind of game is it?

Penguin Sled Rush is a downhill sledding race with a cartoon penguin and a bright snowy town built in 3D. There is no button to hold for speed: the sled picks up speed by itself, and you steer from side to side down a wide ice chute. A run takes about a minute, which makes it easy to try again for a better score.

The course is the **Snowpeak Harbour run**. It starts under a blue START arch in a little village, drops through snowy pine slopes, crosses a frozen canal under stone bridges, winds through a lantern village hung with bunting and finishes with a fast pine run to the FINISH arch.

## Controls

| Action | Phone or tablet | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | Drag left or right anywhere on the screen | Left / Right arrows or A / D |
| Hop | Swipe up, or tap the JUMP button | Space, Up arrow or W |
| Pause | Pause button (top right) | Esc or P |

Steering works like a joystick: put your finger down anywhere, slide it left or right and hold it there to keep turning. Let go and the sled straightens up. The edges of the chute are soft, so scraping a wall just slows you a little.

The game plays in portrait or landscape. If it feels slow on an older phone, open Settings on the title screen and set Graphics to **Low**. **Auto** adjusts the quality by itself.

## What is on the course

- **Golden fish** float in rows along the chute and in arcs above ramps. Each one is worth **10 points**.
- **Blue boost pads** have glowing arrows. Ride over one for a short burst of speed.
- **Ramps** launch you into the air. The wide red-and-white kickers send you highest, often over a curve of fish.
- **Logs** lie right across the chute and **fences** block part of it. Hop over a log, and hop over a fence or steer around it.
- **Snowmen** and **ice blocks** stand in your path. Bumping one costs you a lot of speed and says **Bonk!**, then you get a moment to recover. The run never ends early.

## How your score works

The results card adds up four things:

| Part | How you earn it |
|---|---|
| Fish | 10 points for every golden fish |
| Air style | Points for each long jump, more for longer air time ("Big air!" and "Huge air!") |
| Time bonus | Extra points for every second you finish under the target time |
| Bonks | Not taken from your score, but each bump slows you down, so your time bonus shrinks |

A good score earns **two stars** at 2,800 points and **three stars** at 4,000 points. Your best score, best time and best stars are saved and shown on the title screen.

## Tips for three stars

- Aim for the middle of a fish row, not the first fish. The sled turns smoothly, so starting your turn early catches the whole row.
- Always take boost pads. Speed raises your time bonus, and it carries you further off ramps.
- Line up with ramps early. A "Huge air!" landing also gives you a short speed boost.
- Hop logs a little before you reach them. At top speed the sled covers a lot of ground during a hop.
- Avoiding one snowman is worth more than chasing one fish next to it.

## What testing changed

Before release, Penguin Sled Rush was played start to finish by an automated test driver on phone-sized screens in portrait and landscape, and loaded with damaged and unusual saved games to check that it always starts. Testing led to changes you will notice: the bunting over the village was raised so it never blocks your view, the camera sits higher on tall phone screens so you can see further ahead and the distant mountains were reshaped so they look like mountains rather than spikes.

## Questions players ask

### Is Penguin Sled Rush free?
Yes. It is free to play in your browser with no download and no sign-up.

### Is it suitable for children?
Yes. Bumping a snowman only slows Pip down with a cartoon "Bonk!", nobody gets hurt, and there is no chat.

### The game is slow on my phone. What can I do?
Open Settings on the title screen and set Graphics to Low. Closing other tabs and apps also helps.

### Where is my best score saved?
On your device. Clearing your browser data or using another device starts fresh.

### Is there more than one course?
Not yet. Right now there is one run, Snowpeak Harbour, and you race against the clock and your own best score.
