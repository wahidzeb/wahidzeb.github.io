Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Games by Zaib (zaibgames.com, "the site", "we", "us"), run by Jahan Zaib in Malaysia, handles information when you visit the site and play its games. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • You can play every game without an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other account details.
  • Game progress and settings are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • We use Google Analytics to measure visits to the site (see Analytics).
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising (see below).

Information we collect

No account data

The site has no registration, login, user profiles, chat or comment features. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details or other account data while you browse or play.

Game saves in local storage

Our games use your browser's localStorage to remember things like unlocked levels, coins, high scores, purchased in-game items (bought with in-game coins only) and settings such as sound and controls. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with the game's "Reset progress" option or by clearing your browser's site data for zaibgames.com.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it or add you to any mailing list.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to measure visits, such as how many people visit, which pages and games they open and roughly where they come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and the information it collects is processed by Google. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising and cookies

To keep the games free, we use or may use Google AdSense and other third-party advertising vendors to display ads on the site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info (and www.youronlinechoices.eu in Europe).

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Ad vendors may also use similar technologies such as web beacons, device identifiers and local storage to measure ads and prevent fraud. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and How Google uses cookies in advertising.

If you do not opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The games will still work, although blocking local storage will stop your progress from being saved.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR), when ads are shown we will ask for your consent through a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through that message. Where personalised ads are not allowed, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown.

Children's privacy

Our games are family-friendly and suitable for a general audience, but the site is not directed specifically at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children should ask a parent or guardian before using the internet and emailing anyone, including us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we only hold data you email to us, contact us at the address below to make a request.

Third-party links

The site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we start showing ads or add new features. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
