Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Games by Zaib (zaibgames.com, "the site", "we", "us") handles information when you visit the site and play its games. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • You can play every game without an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other account details.
  • Game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits to the site (see Analytics).
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising (see Advertising and cookies).
  • Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are used (see Consent).

Information we collect

No account data

The site has no registration, login, user profiles, chat or comment features. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details or other account data while you browse or play.

Game saves on your device

Our games use your browser's storage on your device to remember things like unlocked levels, coins, high scores, items bought with in-game coins and settings such as sound and controls. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with a game's reset option or by clearing your browser's site data for zaibgames.com.

Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive your contact details and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it or add you to any mailing list.

Hosting

The site is served by a third-party hosting provider. Like any web host, it may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to measure visits, such as how many people visit, which pages and games they open and roughly where they come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and the information it collects is processed by Google. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising and cookies

To keep the games free, we use or may use Google and other third-party advertising vendors to show ads on the site's pages. Ads are never shown inside or over a game while you play.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info (and www.youronlinechoices.eu in Europe).

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Ad vendors may also use similar technologies such as web beacons, device identifiers and local storage to measure ads and prevent fraud. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and How Google uses cookies in advertising.

If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The games will still work, although blocking site storage stops your progress from being saved.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR), when ads are shown we ask for your consent through a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used. The message lets you accept, decline or choose your options, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the same message. Where personalised ads are not allowed, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown.

Children's privacy

Our games are family-friendly and many are made with children in mind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: there are no accounts, no chat and nothing to type in. Where our pages may be visited by children, we ask our advertising partners to treat those visits appropriately, which can mean showing only non-personalised ads. Children should ask a parent or guardian before contacting anyone online, including us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we only hold information you send us, contact us to make a request.

Third-party links

The site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when ads start appearing or new features are added. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please visit the contact page.
