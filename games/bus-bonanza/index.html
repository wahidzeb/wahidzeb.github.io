Bus Bonanza

About the game

Bus Bonanza is a traffic-jam passenger puzzle for phones and desktop browsers. A long, winding queue of passengers waits in colour runs. Below it, a packed lot holds glossy cars, vans and buses parked at all kinds of angles, and each one has an arrow on its roof. Tap a vehicle to drive it out, park it, and watch the matching passengers walk over and climb aboard.

Hundreds of levels, every one of them solvable, and the difficulty climbs smoothly with a few breather levels mixed in. Every five levels the scenery changes between beach, city, snow and night. You earn coins by winning levels and spend them on power-ups and extra parking spots.

How to play

  1. Tap a vehicle to drive it out of the lot. Vehicles only drive forward, the way their arrow points. If another vehicle is in the way, it bumps and shakes and stays put.
  2. A vehicle that gets out drives around the ring road into a free parking spot. Passengers at the front of the queue board a parked vehicle of their colour. Cars seat 4, vans 6 and buses 10. A full vehicle leaves and frees its spot.
  3. You run out of space when every spot is taken and the next passenger matches none of the parked vehicles. Dig out the colour the queue needs next before you fill your spots with the wrong ones.
  4. Power-ups: VIP flies any vehicle, even a blocked one, to the VIP spot. Arrange sorts the queue. Jumble reshuffles the colours of the vehicles in the lot. Undo takes back a move. The three locked spots can be opened with coins for one level.
  5. If you run out of space, you can undo, continue for coins or retry. Finishing without power-ups earns 3 stars.

Features

  • Hundreds of levels, every one of them solvable, plus level select with stars
  • Cars, vans and buses (4, 6 and 10 seats) at free angles, each with a direction arrow
  • A long, winding passenger queue in colour runs, with cute bobbing passengers who walk over to board
  • A parking row with a VIP spot and three spots you can unlock
  • VIP, Arrange and Jumble power-ups with count badges, plus Undo
  • Beach, city, snow and night themes that change every five levels
  • Glossy pseudo-3D vehicles with soft shadows, bump-and-shake collisions, particles, and music and sound effects
  • Portrait, landscape and desktop layouts, with progress saved on your device

Good to know

Bus Bonanza runs on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser. It is a single web page with no plug-ins. Your progress (levels, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. See our Privacy Policy.
