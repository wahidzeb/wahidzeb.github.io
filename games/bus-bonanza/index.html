About the game

Bus Bonanza is a color-matching bus parking puzzle for phones and desktop browsers. The parking lot is jammed with buses facing every direction. Tap a bus to drive it out to the station, where a line of passengers waits to board the bus that matches their color. When a bus is full it drives away and frees its bay.

There are 120 levels, and a solver bot has checked that every one can be won without boosters. There is also a fresh Daily Challenge each day. Some levels swap the parking lot for a Crowd Rush, where you tap passengers to send them to the arriving buses. Spend the coins you win on bus skins, the City, Beach, Snow and Night themes, and boosters.

How to play

  1. Tap a bus to drive it out of the lot. Buses only drive forward, in the direction of the arrow on the roof. If another bus or obstacle is in the way, it bumps and stays put.
  2. Each bus parks in a free station bay. Passengers at the front of the line board a parked bus of their color, one at a time. A full bus departs and frees its bay.
  3. You lose when every bay is taken and the next passenger matches none of the parked buses, so plan which colors you bring in.
  4. In Crowd Rush levels, tap a passenger who has a clear path to the top. If they match the waiting bus they board it. If not, they sit on the bench, and a full bench ends the level.
  5. Watch for the new mechanics: 4-, 6- and 10-seat buses, grey mystery buses, padlocked buses that need the key bus to leave first, ice that melts as other buses depart, garages that release more buses, VIP passengers worth bonus coins, and timed levels.
  6. Boosters: Extra Bay opens another bay or bench seat, Undo takes back a move, Shuffle rearranges waiting passengers, Magnet pulls matching passengers onto parked buses, and Helicopter airlifts any bus or passenger. Fewer bumps earn more stars.

Features

  • 120 levels, each checked by a solver bot, plus a level map with up to 3 stars per level
  • Two modes: Parking Lot and Crowd Rush
  • Mystery buses, locks and keys, ice, garages, VIP passengers, timed levels and 4-, 6- and 10-seat buses
  • A Daily Challenge generated in your browser, with bonus coins and a streak counter
  • 5 boosters: Extra Bay, Undo, Shuffle, Magnet and Helicopter
  • Shop with 7 bus skins and 4 themes (City, Beach, Snow, Night)
  • Shaded pseudo-3D buses and people, smooth driving animations, combos, confetti, and music and sound effects made in code
  • Tutorial hand hints, sound, music, vibration and graphics settings, with progress saved on your device

Good to know

Bus Bonanza runs on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser. It is a single web page with no plug-ins. Your progress (levels, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. See our Privacy Policy.
