About the game

Moto Mayhem is a road-rash style motorbike racing game for phones and desktop browsers. Every race puts you on a winding highway against seven rival bikers, and the quickest way past them is often a well-timed punch or kick.

Race through five tracks, from the easy curves of Sunset Coast to the icy hairpins of Frost Peak. Win cash, buy faster bikes in the garage and upgrade your engine, tires and armor to climb the standings.

How to play

  1. Throttle is automatic. Steer to stay on the road and dodge traffic and roadside objects.
  2. Ride alongside a rival and attack toward their side. Kicks reach further and hit harder but are slower than punches.
  3. Empty a rival's health bar to knock them off their bike for bonus cash. Grab a club for heavy hits, or knock out a rival carrying one to steal it.
  4. Watch both bars: crashes damage your BIKE (zero means wrecked), and hits drain your RIDER health (zero means you are knocked down and lose time).
  5. From Red Canyon on, a police biker patrols. Get knocked down near him and you are busted with a fine, or knock him out for a big bonus.
  6. Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next track.

Features

  • 5 tracks: Sunset Coast, Pine Valley, Red Canyon, Neon City and Frost Peak, with rising traffic, hills and oncoming cars
  • 7 rival bikers per race with their own health bars and aggression
  • 4 bikes to buy, from the Rustbucket 250 to the Thunderbolt 1200
  • Garage upgrades for engine, tires and armor
  • Pickups: cash, wrench (bike repair), medkit (health), nitro (speed boost) and clubs
  • Police patrols on later tracks
  • Three steering modes (Touch, Drag, Tilt with calibration), vibration and sound settings
  • Progress saved on your device

Good to know

Moto Mayhem runs on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser. It is a single web page with no plug-ins. Your progress (levels, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. See our Privacy Policy.
