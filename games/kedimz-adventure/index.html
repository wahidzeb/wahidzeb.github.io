About the game

Kedimz Adventure is a mobile-first side-scrolling platformer. Kedimz the cat sets out to bring back the Golden Bells across five worlds of four levels each, and every world ends with a boss fight.

Jump, run and paw-swipe through Meadow Hills, Sunscorch Desert, Frostbite Caverns, Ember Castle and Cloud Kingdom. Collect gems and coins, find secret bonus rooms and spend your coins on cat skins, hats and upgrades. All characters, art and music are original.

How to play

  1. Move left and right. Keep holding a direction and Kedimz speeds up into a run.
  2. Press JUMP to jump; hold it for a higher jump. Stomp on enemies or swipe them with your paw.
  3. Break bricks and hit blocks to find coins and power-ups. Some pipes lead to secret bonus rooms.
  4. Damage is forgiving: you shrink first, then lose a heart. Falling into a pit costs a heart and puts you back on safe ground.
  5. Collect 3 gems in each level and beat your best time. 100 coins give an extra life.
  6. Defeat the boss at the end of each world to open the next.

Features

  • 5 worlds and 20 levels with their own scenery, enemies, hazards and music
  • 5 bosses: Mole King, Scarab Pharaoh, Yeti, Magma Golem and Storm Owl
  • Power-ups: Size Up, Fire Paw, Ice Paw, Wings (double jump), Coin Magnet and Star
  • World map with 3 gems per level and best times
  • Shop with cat skins, hats and upgrades
  • Checkpoints, moving and falling platforms, springs, spikes and lava
  • Button or joystick controls, adjustable button size and left-handed layout
  • Progress saved on your device

Good to know

Kedimz Adventure runs on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser. It is a single web page with no plug-ins. Your progress (levels, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. See our Privacy Policy.
