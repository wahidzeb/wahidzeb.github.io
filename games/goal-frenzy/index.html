About the game

Goal Frenzy is an arcade football game with a 3D-perspective stadium, curling shots and over-the-top power shots. Matches are short and fast, and they play well with a thumb on a phone or on a keyboard.

Play a Quick Match against the CPU, take on a friend on the same device, win the 8-team Zaib Cup, run a full League season or settle it in a Penalty Shootout. Create your own club, earn coins and upgrade your squad.

How to play

  1. Move with the floating joystick on the left side of the screen.
  2. Tap SHOOT to shoot; hold it for more power and swipe across the button to curl the ball.
  3. Tap PASS for a ground pass; hold it for a lob or through ball. Hold SPRINT to run faster (uses stamina).
  4. Without the ball, SHOOT becomes SLIDE (tackle) and PASS becomes SWITCH player.
  5. Fill the power meter and press POWER to unleash a special shot such as Fire Shot or Banana Curve.
  6. In penalties, swipe up toward the goal to shoot and tap where you want to dive when you are in goal.

Features

  • Modes: Quick Match, Zaib Cup (knockout), League (7 matchdays) and Penalty Shootout
  • 2 players on one device, each with their own half of the screen (P1 WASD, P2 arrows on keyboard)
  • 5v5 or 7v7, four difficulties from Easy to Legend
  • 7 power shots, including Phantom Shot and Sky Meteor
  • 16 fictional clubs plus your own custom club, kit and badge colours
  • Day, dusk and night matches with slow-motion goal replays
  • Interactive training tutorial and assisted controls
  • Progress saved on your device
