Quick Arcade Games

Arcade games are for when you have a minute or two. Rounds are short, the controls fit on one thumb, and the goal is simple: go a bit further or score a bit more than last time.

Sky Glide is a paper plane with one control: hold to lift and let go to glide, with five worlds from a classroom to outer space. Neon Dash is a one-button runner in a glowing synthwave city, Gobble Hole lets you steer a hungry hole that swallows a city, Fin Frenzy puts you in charge of a hungry shark, and Noodle Clash is a snake-style battle in a giant noodle bowl that two players can share on one phone. Hop Heights adds a 3D obstacle course, and Burger Rush turns a few minutes into a busy shift at a burger diner, where you grill, stack and serve animal customers before their patience runs out.

All of them save your best scores on your device. The guides below share the small tricks, like short taps in Sky Glide, that make the biggest difference.
