Gentle Games for Younger Players

These games are built so younger children can play at their own pace, with big buttons, light reading and nothing scary. When something goes wrong, the result is always kind: a plant just grows a little slower, a pet is a bit hungry, or a giggling raccoon sends you back to the start.

Learn Arabic letters, short surahs, wudu, salah and good manners with Huda and Sami in Noor Quest, our newest learning game. Grow funny plant creatures in Silly Sprout Garden, look after a pet fish in Betta Bestie or a black kitten in Baby Kedim, decorate rooms for clients in Dream Room, run a small farm in Sunny Acres, or make music with Beat Buddies. Critter Keep Rescue adds a little excitement, with sneaking past patrolling raccoons to free critters from cages, and Kedim Therapist is a kind, funny story about a cat who listens to silly patients.

Not every game on the site is aimed at young children, since some are action games. This page collects the ones we would hand to a five- or six-year-old with confidence. Each guide explains the controls and what to expect.
