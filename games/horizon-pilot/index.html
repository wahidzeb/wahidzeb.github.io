About the game

Horizon Pilot is a 3D flying game for phones and desktop browsers. You start in a small trainer at Zaib International and explore an island with mountains, valleys, lakes, a coastline, cities and 7 airports, under day, dusk, night and storm skies.

The career has 25 missions: ring courses, landings and crosswind landings, splashdowns, cargo drops, firefighting, sea rescues, air shows, an engine failure, a jammed landing gear and jet races. Earn coins and XP to rank up, buy new planes, upgrade engines and paint them in new liveries. Free Flight hides 30 golden stars, and there is a new Daily Challenge every day.

How to play

  1. Push the throttle (right slider) up, roll down the runway and gently pull back on the stick to take off.
  2. Drag the stick (left side) to bank and climb or dive. Let go and the assisted controls level the wings for you. Hold the stick all the way to one side for a full roll, or all the way up or down for a loop.
  3. Follow the objective at the top of the screen and the green markers: fly through rings, reach waypoints or find the target.
  4. To land, line up with the runway, lower the GEAR and FLAPS, slow down and follow the glide path dots. Touch down gently and hold BRAKE to stop.
  5. Switch cameras with CAM: chase, cockpit with instruments, or cinematic. AUTO lets the autopilot fly when you need a break (it caps that mission at 2 stars).

Features

  • Real 3D WebGL graphics: terrain, ocean, clouds, cities, airports with runway lights and PAPI, and day, dusk, night and storm skies
  • 7 aircraft: Sparrow trainer, Bumblebee biplane, Pelican floatplane, Hornet aerobatic, Falcon business jet, Atlas airliner and Viper fighter
  • An arcade-sim flight model with assisted controls, stalls, flaps, gear, wind and turbulence
  • 25 career missions with briefings, maps and up to 3 stars each
  • Free Flight with 30 golden stars, plus a Daily Challenge with a streak bonus
  • Upgrades, liveries, smoke colours, coins, XP and ranks
  • Chase, cockpit and cinematic cameras, glide-path guidance, and an interactive tutorial
  • Sound made in code, automatic graphics quality, and progress saved on your device

Good to know

Horizon Pilot runs on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser. It is a single web page with no plug-ins. Your progress (levels, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. See our Privacy Policy.
