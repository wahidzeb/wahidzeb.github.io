About the game

Fin Frenzy is an underwater arcade game for phones and desktop browsers. You play a hungry shark that starts small and grows by eating fish. Your hunger bar keeps dropping, so you have to keep eating, while avoiding predators, mines, jellyfish and harpoons.

Dive through five zones: Coral Reef, Shipwreck Bay, Arctic Ice, Deep Sea and The Abyss, where the only light comes from glowing creatures. Each zone has five levels and ends with a boss fight. Earn coins to unlock new sharks from the Hammerhead to the Megalodon and fit them with gadgets like a jetpack and a laser visor.

How to play

  1. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer with a floating joystick, or switch to follow-finger mode in Settings.
  2. Swim into fish that are smaller than you to eat them. Auto-bite is on by default. Bigger fish glow red: stay away until you have grown.
  3. Keep the hunger bar full. Eating quickly builds a combo, and filling the Frenzy meter makes you faster and lets you eat almost anything for a short time.
  4. Tap BOOST (or double-tap) for a burst of speed and BITE for a strong bite. Boost uses stamina, which refills on its own.
  5. Pick up power-ups such as Magnet, Bubble Shield, Jet Boost, Laser, Mega Bite, Heal and Gold Rush.
  6. Complete each level's goal to earn up to 3 stars. In boss levels, eat and grow first, then bite the boss when it is tired or stunned to deal critical damage.

Features

  • 5 ocean zones with 25 levels, each with a different goal, plus an Endless mode for each zone that saves your best score
  • 5 bosses: King Crab, The Iron Tide submarine, Frost Narwhal, Giant Squid and Abyss Angler
  • 6 sharks to unlock and upgrade: Reef Shark, Hammerhead, Mako, Tiger Shark, Great White and Megalodon
  • Gadgets with 5 upgrade levels each: Jetpack, Laser Visor, Coin Magnet, Armor Plates, Big Belly and Frenzy Charm
  • Combos, Frenzy mode, Gold Rush, treasure chests, missions and Lucky Shell revives
  • Light rays, caustics, parallax scenery and glowing deep-sea creatures, all drawn in code
  • Stick or follow-finger controls, left-handed layout, graphics quality, sound, music and vibration settings
  • Progress saved on your device

Good to know

Fin Frenzy runs on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser. It is a single web page with no plug-ins. Your progress (levels, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. See our Privacy Policy.
