Racing, Driving and Flying Games

If it has wheels or wings, it is here. These five games all put you in control of a vehicle, but they ask for very different skills.

Neon Nitro is our newest racer: bright cartoon street cars, near misses in traffic, drifting, nitro and patrol cars to escape across 18 events in three districts. Moto Mayhem is a fast motorbike race against seven rivals, with cartoon punches and kicks and five tracks from Sunset Coast to Frost Peak. Haulway Heroes is the calm one: a 3D truck-driving game where smooth driving and good parking earn the best pay. Horizon Pilot is a flight game on a 3D island with seven airports and assisted controls, so a child can take off on the first try. Zaib City is an open 3D city to explore on foot and by car, where you earn money driving taxis, delivering pizza and putting out fires.

The 3D games need a reasonably recent phone, and quality adjusts automatically. Moto Mayhem includes cartoon fighting between riders, so it suits older children best.
