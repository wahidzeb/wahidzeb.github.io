About the game

Zaib City is a family-friendly 3D open-world city game for phones and desktop browsers. The bright low-poly city has a downtown of glass towers, a leafy suburb, a sunny beach with a boardwalk pier and a harbour, an industrial zone, a big park with a lake, a highway loop and Sunset Hill with stunt ramps. Time flows from sunrise to starry night, and the weather changes between sunshine, clouds, rain and fog.

Everyone in Zaib City is friendly. Walk up to any parked car or any car in traffic: the driver politely stops, steps out, waves and lends it to you. Traffic follows the roads and the traffic lights, pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and jump aside, and bumps are always harmless and funny. Instead of a wanted level you build Reputation by driving nicely, waiting at red lights and helping people.

Earn cash and XP with taxi fares, pizza and parcel deliveries, ambulance runs to the hospital, fire truck call-outs with a water cannon, police speed patrols where you bump speeders to pull them over, street races, time trials, parking challenges, a bus route and stunt shows. A 22-mission story with friendly characters unlocks new districts. Spend your cash on 14 cars at the dealership, paint and upgrades at the garage, and homes that work as save points.

How to play

  1. On foot, drag the left side of the screen to walk (push further to run) and tap JUMP. Drag the right side to look around.
  2. Tap ENTER next to any car to get in. Traffic drivers stop, hop out and wave. Tap EXIT to get out.
  3. Drive with the left and right arrows, GAS and BRAKE (hold BRAKE to reverse). Hold DRIFT while turning to slide, and press NITRO for a boost. Tilt or stick steering is in Settings.
  4. Follow the pink GPS line on the minimap. Gold ★ markers are story missions and coloured beams are jobs. Drive into a marker and tap the button that appears.
  5. Open the phone for jobs, the story list, car delivery, homes, districts, daily challenges and awards. On the big map, tap anywhere to set a waypoint.
  6. Visit Sprocket's Garage to repair, paint and upgrade, Zaib Motors to buy cars, and homes to rest and save. Find the hidden stars and land every stunt jump.
