Puzzle and Brain Games

These are the games where thinking ahead matters more than fast fingers. Each one is built around a single clear idea, and the levels slowly ask you to plan a little further.

In Bus Bonanza you free vehicles from a packed car park in the order the passenger queue needs them. In Rail Rush you tap switches so each train reaches the station of its colour, and every station has a symbol as well as a colour. Juicy Merge drops fruit into a jar, where matching pieces merge into bigger fruit. Block Glide slides clay blocks through matching gates, Kedim's Snack Swing asks you to cut ropes so a treat swings into a hungry cat's mouth, and in Number Dash you steer a runner through gates that add, take away, multiply or divide so you finish bigger than the Number Monster.

Most levels are short, so these games fit into a spare few minutes, and every one saves your progress on your device. Each guide below explains the rules and the habits that help you earn three stars.
