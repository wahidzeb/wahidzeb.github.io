Kedim's Brain Doodles Guide: All 4 Packs, Free Hints and Three Stars

Kedim's Brain Doodles is a brain teaser game with Kedim, the orange cat. Every level is one tricky doodle picture set somewhere in Malaysia, with a short question at the top. The answer is rarely the obvious one, so read carefully, look closely and think a little sideways.

By Zaib · Updated 26 November 2026

PLAY KEDIM'S BRAIN DOODLES

What kind of game is it?

Kedim's Brain Doodles is a set of 40 short brain teasers drawn like doodles in a school exercise book. Each puzzle is one picture and one question, such as "Kedim is hungry! Which one is NOT food?" or "Kedim counted 5 stars, but there are only 4 in the sky. Tap the 5th star!" The places are familiar Malaysian ones: a mamak stall, a kampung on stilts, the beach at night, a kitchen and a Hari Raya evening.

Kedim sits beside every puzzle and talks to you in a speech bubble. He cheers when you get it right, gives a gentle nudge when you get it wrong, and shares a small fun fact after each solve. There is no timer, the hints are always free and the game never asks for money.

The four packs

The 40 puzzles come in 4 packs of 10. Each pack teaches one new trick, and a card at the start of the pack explains it.

Inside a pack, the levels open one after another. Finish 7 levels in a pack and the next pack opens, so you can try a new trick before you have three stars everywhere.

How stars work

Every puzzle gives up to 3 stars, and the best result is saved:

  • 3 stars: solved with no wrong tries.
  • 2 stars: one or two wrong tries.
  • 1 star: three or more wrong tries.

There are 120 stars in total. Using a hint never costs a star, so it is always fine to ask Kedim for help.

Free hints and read-aloud

Tap Hint once and Kedim shows a hint and reads it out loud. Tap it again and the game also highlights the part of the picture to look at. The speaker button reads the question aloud, which helps younger players who are still learning to read.

Tips for tricky puzzles

  1. Read every word. Words like NOT, BIGGEST and SMALLEST are in capitals for a reason, and the trick often hides in the question itself.
  2. Look at the whole picture, including the sky, the clouds and the edges. In "Tap the hiding cat", the cat is not where you expect.
  3. Try moving things in the Move It! pack. If a puzzle seems impossible, something in the picture can usually be dragged.
  4. Draw big and clear in the Draw It In pack. The game checks that your line is in the right place, not how neat it is.
  5. For One Line puzzles, start on a dot with an odd number of lines. If every dot has an even number of lines, you can start anywhere. Plan where you will finish before you start.

Questions players ask

Is Kedim's Brain Doodles free?

Yes. All 40 puzzles are free to play in the browser, with no download, no account and no ads during play.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It was made for kids and families: no timers, no scary content, free hints and a read-aloud button for every question.

Where is my progress saved?

Your stars are saved on your device in this browser. If you clear your browsing data, the progress is reset.

I am stuck on a level. What can I do?

Tap Hint twice: the first tap reads a hint aloud and the second shows where to look. Hints are free and never cost a star.

Is there music?

No. The game has sound effects only, and you can turn them off in Settings.
