Penguin Sled Rush Guide: Rivals, Courses, Ghost Runs and Three Stars

Penguin Sled Rush is a 3D sledding race. Pip the penguin rides a red wooden sled against three rival penguins on three courses: Snowpeak Harbour, Aurora Night Run and Frozen Waterfall. The sled goes forward by itself, so every run is about steering, timing your hops and choosing the best line for fish, boosts and big air.

What kind of game is it?

Penguin Sled Rush is a downhill sledding race with a cartoon penguin and a bright snowy town built in 3D. There is no button to hold for speed: the sled picks up speed by itself, and you steer from side to side down a wide ice chute. A run takes about a minute, which makes it easy to try again for a better score.

You never race alone. Three rival penguins, Pebble, Juniper and Biscuit, start beside you, and the badge in the top left shows your place from 1st to 4th as you go. Small dots on the progress bar show where the rivals are.

Steering works like a joystick: put your finger down anywhere, slide it left or right and hold it there to keep turning. Let go and the sled straightens up. The edges of the chute are soft, so scraping a wall just slows you a little.

The game plays in portrait or landscape. If it feels slow on an older phone, open Settings on the title screen and set Graphics to Low. Auto adjusts the quality by itself.

What is on the course

  • Golden fish float in rows along the chute and in arcs above ramps. Each one is worth 10 points.
  • Blue boost pads have glowing arrows. Ride over one for a short burst of speed.
  • Ramps launch you into the air. The wide red-and-white kickers send you highest, often over a curve of fish.
  • Logs lie right across the chute and fences block part of it. Hop over a log, and hop over a fence or steer around it.
  • Snowmen and ice blocks stand in your path. Bumping one costs you a lot of speed and says Bonk!, then you get a moment to recover. The run never ends early. Rivals bump into things too, and they wobble just like you.

How your score works

The results card adds up four things:

PartHow you earn it
Fish10 points for every golden fish
Air stylePoints for each long jump, more for longer air time ("Big air!" and "Huge air!")
Time bonusExtra points for every second you finish under the target time
BonksNot taken from your score, but each bump slows you down, so your time bonus shrinks

The star targets for each course are in the table above, and the results card shows them under your stars, so you can see how close you came. When you beat your best score on a course, the card says New best!

The results card also shows your place, from 1st to 4th, and the finishing order with everyone's time. Your place does not change your score, but the daily goals sometimes ask for a top-two finish.

Ghost runs

Each course keeps a ghost of your fastest finish, saved on your device. Next time you race that course, a see-through penguin follows your old line so you can see whether you are ahead or behind. Beat its time and your new run becomes the ghost. To race without it, tap Ghost: On on the course list, or change it in Settings.

Daily goals and the fish shop

Every day you get three daily goals, such as "Grab 120 fish in one run", "Finish in the top 2 twice" or "Earn 3 stars in one run". Tap Daily on the title screen to see your progress. Each finished goal pays bonus fish, and new goals arrive the next day.

The golden fish you collect are kept as a wallet. Spend them in the Shop on sled colours, beanies and scarves. They change only how Pip looks. Nothing in the shop makes you faster.

Tips for three stars

  • Aim for the middle of a fish row, not the first fish. The sled turns smoothly, so starting your turn early catches the whole row.
  • Always take boost pads. Speed raises your time bonus, and it carries you further off ramps.
  • Line up with ramps early. A "Huge air!" landing also gives you a short speed boost.
  • Hop logs a little before you reach them. At top speed the sled covers a lot of ground during a hop.
  • Avoiding one snowman is worth more than chasing one fish next to it.

What testing changed

Before release, Penguin Sled Rush was played start to finish by an automated test driver on phone-sized screens in portrait and landscape, and loaded with damaged and unusual saved games to check that it always starts. Testing led to changes you will notice: the bunting over the village was raised so it never blocks your view, the camera sits higher on tall phone screens so you can see further ahead and the distant mountains were reshaped so they look like mountains rather than spikes. In the November update the test driver also raced every course against the rivals, on slow, throttled computers as well as fast ones, which is how the star targets for the new courses were set.

Questions players ask

Is Penguin Sled Rush free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser with no download and no sign-up.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. Bumping a snowman only slows Pip down with a cartoon "Bonk!", nobody gets hurt, and there is no chat.

The game is slow on my phone. What can I do?

Open Settings on the title screen and set Graphics to Low. Closing other tabs and apps also helps.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device: scores, stars, unlocked courses, ghosts, daily goals, fish and outfits. Clearing your browser data or using another device starts fresh.

How many courses are there?

Three. Snowpeak Harbour is open from the start, Aurora Night Run unlocks at 2 stars and Frozen Waterfall at 5 stars.

Can I lose a race by crashing?

No. A bump only slows you down for a moment. Every run reaches the finish, and you can still win after a bonk.

Can I buy speed with fish?

No. Fish buy sled colours, beanies and scarves, which change only how Pip looks.

Is there music?

No. The game has sound effects only, and you can switch them off in Settings.
