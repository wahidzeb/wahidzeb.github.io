Kedai Tile Trip Guide: Clear All 36 Levels and Fill the Kedai Collection

Kedai Tile Trip is a calm triple tile matching puzzle with Kedim the orange cat. Tap clay tiles of kuih, wau kites, batik and Raya treats into your tray, and every three of the same tile clear away. Clear the whole pile before the tray fills up.

By Zaib · Updated 26 November 2026

PLAY KEDAI TILE TRIP

What kind of game is it?

Kedai Tile Trip is a triple tile matching game set in a little Malaysian shop, or kedai. Each level is a pile of chunky clay tiles showing things you would find in one: kuih lapis and onde-onde, wau kites, batik patterns, ketupat and pelita lamps. Below the pile is a wooden tray with seven slots.

Tap a tile and it slides into the tray. When three of the same tile meet in the tray, they clear with a pop. Clear every tile on the board to win. If the tray fills up with seven tiles that do not match, the level is lost, but the helpers make that rare.

How to play

  1. Tap a free tile on the pile to move it into the tray. Free tiles are bright. Darker tiles are covered by something on top.
  2. Make three of a kind. Three matching tiles in the tray clear away and make room.
  3. Plan ahead. Before you tap, look for a tile type that has three free copies, or one that already has two in the tray.
  4. Clear the board before the seven slots fill up.

Levels 1 to 5 cannot be lost, so new players can learn the rules in peace. Covered tiles start at level 6, and padlocked tiles arrive at level 15: a padlock opens after a set number of matches.

The three helpers

Every level gives you a fresh set of free helpers:

HelperWhat it does
UndoTakes back your last tile.
ShuffleMixes the tiles on the board into new places.
Send backSends 3 tiles from the tray up to a shelf, where you can tap them again later.

Send back is the best rescue when the tray is nearly full. Shuffle helps when no free tiles match.

How stars work

  • 3 stars: cleared with no helpers.
  • 2 stars: one or two helpers used.
  • 1 star: three or more helpers used.

There are 108 stars over 36 levels, and your best result on each level is saved.

Challenge mode

Once you know the game, switch the level map to Challenge. The same levels get a timer, worked out from the number of tiles. If time runs out, you can try again or go back to Relax mode, which never has a timer. Your best times are saved.

The Kedai Collection

Every tile type you clear goes into the Kedai Collection, an album of 24 items in four sets: Kuih Corner, Wau Shop, Batik Stall and Raya Shelf. Tap an item to read a short fact about it, such as which kuih is steamed in layers or what a wau bulan is.

Tips for three stars

  1. Keep the tray light. Try to keep at least three slots empty, so one unlucky tile cannot end the level.
  2. Finish pairs first. If a tile type already has two in the tray, look for the third before starting a new type.
  3. Uncover the dark tiles. Clear the tiles on top of big covered stacks early, so you have more choices later.
  4. Count the padlocks. Locked tiles open after a number of matches, so plan some easy matches to open them.
  5. Save Send back for emergencies. One helper still gives you 2 stars.

Questions players ask

Is Kedai Tile Trip free?

Yes. All 36 levels and both modes are free in the browser, with no download, no account and no ads during play.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. There is no timer in Relax mode, the first five levels cannot be lost and the helpers are free in every level.

Where is my progress saved?

Your stars, best times and collection are saved on your device in this browser.

Is there music?

No. The game has sound effects only, and you can turn them off.
