Tank Rumble Guide: Keep Rolling, Pick Smart Powers and Beat the Boss Bots

Tank Rumble is a cartoon battle on a toy-room floor. You drive a little green toy tank around a play mat while it fires by itself, popping robot toys into bolts and coins. Our first game ended on stage 1-1. The second got further, and taught us what really matters.

By Zaib · Updated 20 October 2026 · Free to play, no download

PLAY TANK RUMBLE

Tank Rumble takes place in a child's bedroom. The arena is a blue play mat on a wooden floor, with giant toy letter blocks (A, B, C, X, Y) to drive around. Your tank is a chunky green toy, and the enemies are wind-up robots and toy tanks. When they are hit, they pop into bolts, gears and coins. There is nothing scary or gory: it is toys against toys.

PLAY TANK RUMBLE

Our first two games

Game one. We pressed PLAY and drove in short straight bursts: right, up, left, down, stopping between each. The tank's cannon picked targets by itself, but every time we paused, the robots' shots caught up with us. We were "Tank Toppled!" on stage 1-1, with 7 robots popped and 6 coins. The card's tip: "upgrade your tank in the Garage!"

Game two. This time we kept moving in smooth circles around the mat. Wave 1 went much better. In wave 2 the enemy tanks and a pink robot fired glowing shots from different sides, and our health dropped to 37, then 25 out of 100, but we cleared it. "Stage clear! Pick a power" offered three cards: Ricochet (shells bounce off walls), Side Cannons (also fire left and right) and Big Shells (+30% damage). We took one and rolled into stage 1-2 with only a quarter of our health left, and were toppled there with 20 robots popped and 17 coins.

The two lessons:

  1. Never stop moving. The cannon aims for you, so all your attention can go on dodging.
  2. Only a little health comes back between stages (15% of your maximum). If you are low, the Repair Kit power (heal 50%) is often the best pick, and red heart pickups restore 35%.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to drive. The tank goes the way you drag.
  • Your tank aims and fires by itself. You can switch aiming in the menu, but Auto is easiest.
  • Pick up the coins and power-ups that robots drop.
  • Each chapter has 5 stages. Stages 1 and 2 have 2 waves, stages 3 and 4 have 3, and stage 5 is the boss fight.

How stars are earned

You earn stars for winning a chapter (beating its boss):

  • 1 star for winning,
  • 2 stars if the total damage you took was less than one and a half times your max health,
  • 3 stars if it was less than half your max health.

So 3 stars means getting through all five stages, boss included, while barely being hit. Winning also pays a coin bonus that grows with each chapter.

The Garage

Coins buy upgrades and new tanks.

Upgrades (10 levels each): Cannon (+15% damage), Armour (+12% health), Engine (+5% speed) and Reload (+8% fire rate). The first level costs 60 coins, then 87, 126, 183 and so on, getting pricier each time.

Our tip: put your first coins into Armour. More health means you finish more stages, and finishing stages earns more coins for everything else.

Tips for parents

Tank Rumble is a simple one-finger action game. The theme is toys and robots, nothing realistic. Rounds are short, and losing just shows a friendly "Tank Toppled!" card with a Play again button.

FAQ

Do I have to aim?

No. Aim is set to Auto, so your tank picks targets and fires by itself.

How many chapters are there?

Four, each with five stages and a boss.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device.
