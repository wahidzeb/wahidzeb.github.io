Moto Mayhem Guide: Stay on the Road, Win Races, Upgrade Your Bike

Moto Mayhem is an arcade motorbike race down a winding highway against seven rival riders. The throttle is automatic, so the game is all about steering, dodging and, when a rival gets too close, a well-timed shove. Our first race taught us the most important rule the hard way.

By Zaib · Updated 20 October 2026 · Free to play, no download

PLAY MOTO MAYHEM

Moto Mayhem looks great on a phone. The first track, Sunset Coast, runs along a sandy shore with palm trees, a huge orange sun and red-and-white kerbs. Your rider sits right in front of you in a yellow helmet, the speedometer reads out km/h, and seven rivals are somewhere up the road.

It is a racing game with cartoon biker scuffles: you can push rivals off their bikes with punches and kicks, and a police biker turns up on later tracks. There is no blood, but it is an action game about knocking riders down, so it suits older children best.

PLAY MOTO MAYHEM

Our first race: a lesson in staying on the road

We started on Sunset Coast in 8th place out of 8. Steering with the arrow keys, we drifted to the left side and onto the sand within the first few seconds. We threw some punches and kicks at empty air, picked up $100 from a pickup, and a minute later we were... still 8th, at a steady 76 km/h.

The reason is simple: riding off the road limits your top speed. The bike slows down, shakes, kicks up dust, and an "OFF ROAD! Steer back" message flashes up. All the rivals were on the tarmac pulling away.

So the first rule of Moto Mayhem: keep the bike between the kerbs. Overtaking and fighting come second.

Your two health bars

  • RIDER (green) drops when rivals hit you. At zero you are knocked down and lose time.
  • BIKE (blue) drops when you crash into traffic or roadside objects. At zero your bike is wrecked.

Pickups help: a wrench repairs the bike, a medkit restores rider health, nitro gives a speed boost, and cash is cash.

Racing tips

  1. Stay on the road. Off-road riding caps your speed.
  2. Brake before sharp bends rather than sliding wide onto the sand.
  3. Dodge traffic first, fight second. A crash costs more time than a rival gains.
  4. Kicks reach further and hit harder; punches are quicker. Use kicks when a rival is a little way off, punches when they are right beside you.
  5. Knocking a rival off their bike pays bonus cash. You can grab a club for heavier hits, or knock out a rival carrying one to take it.
  6. Watch for the police biker from Red Canyon on. Get knocked down near him and you are busted with a fine. Knock him out and you get a big bonus.

Prizes

Every place pays something on the first track: $1,500 for 1st, then $1,000, $700, $500, $350, $250, $150 and $100 for 8th. Later tracks multiply the prizes (Pine Valley's first prize is $2,400, Red Canyon's $3,600 and Neon City's $5,100). Finishing in the top 3 unlocks the next track.

What changed after testing

Updates gave the game a graphics pass and general polish, then made big cash amounts display in a compact way (K, M and so on) so they always fit on screen.

FAQ

Why am I so slow?

Check you are on the road. Off-road riding caps your speed.

Do I have to fight?

No. You can win just by riding well, but knocking rivals down pays extra and clears the way.

How do I unlock Pine Valley?

Finish in the top 3 on Sunset Coast.

Is progress saved?

Yes, your cash, bikes and upgrades are saved on your device.
