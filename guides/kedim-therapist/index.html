Kedim Therapist Guide: Best Replies, Mini-Games and 5-Star Sessions

In Kedim Therapist you are Dr. Kedim, a ginger cat with a red scarf who listens to silly animal patients. Each session is a short, funny chat plus one mini-game. This guide shows how the mood meter really works, so you can turn 3-star visits into 5-star ones.

By Zaib · Updated 20 October 2026

PLAY KEDIM THERAPIST

Kedim Therapist is a cosy comedy game set in a little office with a couch, an armchair and a rubber duck wandering about. Your patients are animals with very silly worries: a dog who is scared of the mail slot, a ghost who is afraid of the dark, a dragon who keeps setting the couch on fire. You listen, pick a reply, play a short calming mini-game and send them home feeling a bit better.

It is gentle and funny rather than serious, though every session ends with a small, real-life tip, such as "Slow breaths tell your body it's safe. Try in for 4, out for 6." Sessions take about two minutes, which makes it a nice game for short breaks.

How a session goes

  1. The patient explains their problem. Tap the speech box to move through the chat. Lines type out like a typewriter, and tapping skips ahead.
  2. You pick a reply. Three cards appear, each labelled Kind, Wise or Funny.
  3. A mini-game. A card explains it, then you tap Start.
  4. A second reply.
  5. Results. The final mood sets your star rating, the patient leaves a short review, and you earn coins and a new page in Kedim's Notebook.

Two stars or more moves the story on to that patient's next session. Each of the 12 patients has three sessions, so there are 36 in the story, and the third one ends with a little graduation.

On a phone everything is tapping, plus dragging in a couple of the mini-games. On a computer, Space or Enter moves the chat along and 1, 2 or 3 picks a reply.

The secret of the mood meter

The bar at the top of the screen is the patient's mood. Here is how it adds up, worked out from playing and checking the numbers:

Our very first session with Biscuit the dog shows the maths. We picked Kind both times. The first Kind reply was the best one (+22), but the second was only okay (+11): the best reply there was the Wise one about watching the mailman from the window with a toy. We scored 50% on the breathing game (+15). That is 22 + 22 + 15 + 11 = 70% mood, which gave 3 stars and 56 coins.

To reach 5 stars with an empty office you need both best replies and at least 80% in the mini-game. Decorating the office and the Tea Machine make it much easier, because the patient starts happier.

Picking the right reply

  • Funny is never the best reply. We checked all 36 story sessions: the best answer is always Kind or Wise, roughly half each. Funny replies are there for the jokes, and they are worth tapping once to see the silly result (feathers everywhere, a scorched cat, a patient running out the door).
  • Kind is not always right either. Ask yourself what the patient actually needs. If they are upset, the kind reply that says their feelings make sense usually wins. If they are stuck on a plan, the wise reply with a small, practical step often wins.
  • The order is shuffled, so the best reply is not always in the same place.
  • Stuck? Use an Insight Nap. The 💤 button at the top makes Kedim nap and highlight the best reply. You start with three, so save them for answers you really can't decide. The Nap Pillow upgrade gives an extra one every new day.

Each patient's three sessions tell a small story. Biscuit's go from "Special Delivery Dread" to "The Package Incident" and finally "Dear Mr. Mailman".

Coins, the shop and upgrades

Coins come from every session, and there is a bonus for better reviews. The Shop has three kinds of things to buy:

- Office decorations such as couches, plants, lamps, rugs, an aquarium and a cat tree. They show up in the office, and many of them add cosiness, which raises every patient's starting mood (up to +14). - Outfits and fur colours for Kedim. These are just for fun. - Upgrades: - Tea Machine: +5 starting mood per level, 3 levels - Cozy Blanket: silly replies cost half as much mood - White Noise Machine: mini-games give 10% more mood per level, 2 levels - Treat Jar: +15% coins per level, 3 levels - Nap Pillow: one extra Insight Nap each new day

What to buy first: the first Tea Machine level (120 coins) is the best early buy because it helps every single session. After that, a cosy couch or plant, then the Treat Jar to speed up coins.

Walk-ins and the daily patient

After your first two story sessions, the Walk-in Clinic opens. It sends random patients one after another, so you can keep playing after the story. There is also a daily patient who pays double coins and builds a streak if you visit each day.

Little things to try in the office

Tap Kedim to pet him. Tap the things on his desk and he knocks them off, just like a real cat (there is an award for doing it 10 times). Ducky the duck and Mei Mei the black cat wander around the office and react to your patients; you can switch them off in Settings. Settings also has a moonlit Midnight office theme.

What we changed after testing

  • Feelings Match got fairer. Mismatched cards now stay up a little longer (0.9 seconds) so you can remember them, the first session has a longer timer, and running out of time shows a proper retry screen.
  • A low-time warning (red pulsing bar and "Hurry!") was added to every timed mini-game.
  • The office came to life: a plant sways on the windowsill, a bird flies past the window, and dust sparkles in the light.
  • Results screens were tidied up so awards show inside the card after the stars, and everything fits on short, wide phone screens.
  • Saved progress was made sturdier so a story can never skip ahead or break.

FAQ

Is Kedim Therapist suitable for kids?

Yes. The worries are silly (a pigeon who doesn't trust bread), the humour is gentle, and nobody gets hurt. The small tips at the end of sessions, like slow breathing, are friendly ideas anyone can use.

What happens if I get a bad rating?

Nothing bad. With 1 star the story simply stays on that session, and you can try again with different replies. You still earn some coins, just fewer than for a passing session.

How many patients and sessions are there?

12 patients with three sessions each, so 36 story sessions, plus endless walk-ins and a daily patient.

What is the fastest way to get 5 stars?

Buy the Tea Machine and some cosy decorations, choose Kind or Wise (never Funny) and aim for 80% or more in the mini-game. Use an Insight Nap when you are unsure.

Does my progress save?

Yes, automatically on your device. Close the game whenever you like and your office, coins and story will be waiting.
