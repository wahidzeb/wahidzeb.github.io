Dream Room Guide: Read the Brief, Place Every Piece and Get 3 Stars

Dream Room is a calm interior design game. Clients send you a short brief, and you pick the bed, walls, floor, art and more to match their style, colours and budget, then drag every piece to just the right spot. Here is how the scoring works, what earns 3 stars and everything you can do between rooms.

By Zaib · Updated 25 October 2026

PLAY DREAM ROOM

Dream Room has a soft, storybook look. Each room is drawn in gentle cartoon style, with glowing + signs where furniture can go. Tap one, browse the pieces, pick a colour and watch the room change. Drag pieces around until the room feels right, then tap Done for the before and after reveal and the client's reaction.

There are no timers and nothing to buy with real money, so it is a relaxing pick for children and adults who like making things look nice. Your coins, rooms and furniture are saved on your device, and players from before the big update keep all their coins, stars and finished rooms.

PLAY DREAM ROOM

How to play

  1. Read the brief. It shows the client's favourite style, their colours, the budget, the room and the must-haves.
  2. Tap a glowing + to choose that spot. Cards along the bottom show the pieces that fit there, with their price.
  3. Pick a piece, then a colour. The colour dots appear after you choose a piece.
  4. Move it. Drag any piece to a new place. Tap ⇋ to flip it the other way, or 🗑 to take it away.
  5. Tap Done when every spot is filled to see the reveal, your stars and the coins you earned.

The first room is a guided one: a little hand shows each step, including one drag, and following it gets you 3 stars. On a phone you can also pinch to zoom in, swipe to look around while zoomed in, tap 🔍 to see the whole room again and 🧊 to turn the view.

Hearts and asking for help

In the first six rooms, little ♥ hearts show the pieces and colours the client will love. After that, tap the client's face to ask for help: hearts then appear for the spot you have chosen. You can ask three times in each room, and the Mood board wall in your studio adds one more.

How scoring works

Your client mood (0 to 100%) is made from four things:

PartWeightWhat it measures
🎨 Style40%How well each piece matches the client's style
🌈 Colours26%How well the colours match the client's palette
✔ Must-haves20%Whether you included everything on the list
🏠 Spots14%How many spots you filled

Then:

  • Thrifty +2 if you fill every spot and spend no more than 85% of the budget.
  • Little touches, up to +3: extra pieces such as books, plants, pets and festive decorations.
  • A bit crowded: pieces pushed right on top of each other cost 2 points each (4 at most). A red glow shows which ones are too close.

Stars: 50% mood gives 1 star and 72% gives 2 stars. 3 stars need 88% mood or more and a style score of 80% or more, colours of 70% or more and every must-have. Missing a must-have caps you at 1 star, however good the room looks. If you get 2 stars, the result card tells you what to change for 3.

Budget rules

You can't finish a room over budget. If you try, the game asks you to swap a piece for a cheaper one. The counter at the top shows what you have spent. The quickest fix is usually the most expensive piece.

Chapters and clients

There are 32 client rooms in five chapters:

ChapterRooms
Fresh Starts1 to 6
Family Homes7 to 12
Open for Business13 to 18
Free Style19 to 25
Dream Estates26 to 32

Clients come back later in the story, so you see Mia go from her first flat to a seaside home and Leo grow from a playroom to a sky explorer. Later rooms are multi-room homes, where one brief covers several rooms. With 3 stars per room there are 96 stars to collect.

Coins and the Catalogue

Finishing rooms earns coins. Spend them in the Catalogue on special furniture, and on festive decoration sets. Some pieces are locked at first and open as you finish more rooms; a lock shows how many rooms you need.

Daily Brief

Every day there is a new Daily Brief from one of your clients. Playing on a new day also gives a coin gift, which grows a little for each day in a row, with a bigger gift every seventh day you play. Missed days never take your coins or rooms away.

My Room, Stickers and Portfolio

  • My Room: decorate any room you like, with no brief and no budget, and keep up to six designs.
  • Stickers: fill a room with toys, pets, plants and decorations for a fun sticker scene.
  • Portfolio: every finished client room, so you can look back at your work.

Design Journal, Style Book and your Studio

  • The Design Journal has friendly goals, such as dragging a piece, giving a room a pet or playing Daily Briefs on five days. Each one gives coins when you finish it.
  • The Style Book has a badge for each of nine styles, from Scandi and Boho to Glam and Rustic. Earn 3 stars in rooms of that style to fill it in.
  • Your Studio is your own design office. Spend coins on six upgrades, from a proper desk to a grand chandelier. Some give a bonus, like an extra hint in every room, more coins from rooms or a golden frame for your photos.

Tips for 3 stars

  1. Check the must-haves first. Missing one caps you at 1 star.
  2. Stay in the client's style. Style counts the most, and 3 stars need 80% or more.
  3. Follow the hearts, and ask the client when they are not shown.
  4. Match the colour palette after every piece.
  5. Leave 15% of the budget for the Thrifty bonus, but still fill every spot.
  6. Add a few little touches, and keep big pieces apart so nothing glows red.

FAQ

Is Dream Room good for kids?

Yes. There are no timers and no failing, just redesigning until the client is happy. The first room teaches every step with a little hand.

Can I redo a room?

Yes. Tap Redesign on the result card, or pick the room again from the map. Your best stars are kept.

I played before the update. Is my progress still there?

Yes. Your coins, stars, finished rooms and the furniture you bought all carry over the first time you open the new version on the same device.

Are my rooms and coins saved?

Yes. Your coins, finished rooms, designs and the furniture you have bought are kept on this device, so you can keep decorating later.
