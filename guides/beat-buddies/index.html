Beat Buddies Guide: Build a Song, Find Secret Combos and Unlock Every Buddy

Beat Buddies is a music toy for kids. Drag cute, round buddies onto a stage and each one starts singing, drumming or beatboxing, always in time, so every mix sounds good. Our five-buddy song set off a secret combo called Starry Night. Here is how to build your own.

By Zaib · Updated 20 October 2026

PLAY BEAT BUDDIES

Beat Buddies needs no reading, no rules and no wrong answers. There is a stage with seven spots, and below it a tray of buddies: red, yellow, blue, pink, round, tall and fluffy, each wearing something silly, like headphones, a cap or a tiny crown. Drag one onto the stage and it joins the song.

Because every loop is made to fit with every other loop, there is no way to make a bad song. That makes it a lovely first music game for small children, and a fun toy for grown-ups too.

Our first song

We pressed Let's Jam! and landed on the Sunny Jam stage: blue sky, green hills and a wooden floor. Simply opening a jam completed a Daily Fun task ("Jam on the Sunny Jam stage", +10 notes).

We built the song one buddy at a time:

  1. Thumpy (kick drum: "boom, boom, boom!") on the back row.
  2. Wubbo (a warm, wobbly synth bass) next to him.
  3. Twinkle (sparkly bells) from the Melody tab.
  4. Fluffy (soft, cosy cloud chords) from the Chords tab.
  5. Sparkle (shimmering chimes) from the Effects tab.

The moment Sparkle landed, the whole stage filled with stars and a banner appeared: SECRET COMBO! Starry Night. That earned 5 notes, plus another Daily Fun task ("Find any secret combo", +10). In under a minute we had gone from 10 notes to 25.

The lesson: use the category tabs (Beats, Bass, Melody, Chords, Voices, Effects). One buddy from each makes a full-sounding song, and that is also how most secret combos are found.

How to play

  • Drag a buddy from the tray onto any empty spot on the stage.
  • Drag it off the stage to remove it.
  • Tap a buddy on stage to Mute it (it falls asleep) or Solo it (hear it on its own).
  • Record with the red button. A song can be up to 2 minutes long.
  • Play it back, and Share to make a link anyone can open.
  • The bin button clears the stage.

Stages

Notes also unlock new stages, which change the backdrop:

  • Sunny Jam (free)
  • Space Groove (15 notes)
  • Candy Pop (30)
  • Spooky Cute (45, spooky colours but still cute)

Daily Fun

Each day brings a few small tasks worth 10 notes each. They come from a list such as:

  • Fill all 7 spots on the stage
  • Put 2 buddies from the same group on stage together
  • Jam on a particular stage
  • Find any secret combo
  • Record a song of at least 10 seconds
  • Solo a buddy
  • Jam for 2 minutes
  • Play with a particular buddy
  • Make a share link for your song

Daily Fun is the fastest way to earn notes, so check it each time you play.

Tips for parents

  • There is no failing and no timer, so it is good for very young children.
  • Muting and soloing buddies is a gentle introduction to how songs are built from layers.
  • The game works offline once it has loaded.
  • A share link stores the song inside the link itself. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.

Quick questions

Is Beat Buddies free?

Yes. Notes are earned by playing, not bought.

How many buddies can be on stage?

Seven at once.

Are my buddies and combos remembered?

Yes. Your notes, the buddies you have bought and the secret combos you have found are kept on this device, so your collection keeps growing each time you play.
