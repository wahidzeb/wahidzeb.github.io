What's New in November: An About Page in Every Game, and One Quick Tip From Each

Every one of our 46 games now has an About page on its title screen, with how to play, tips from Zaib and four similar games. Here is how to find it, what else is new, and one quick tip from every game.

By Zaib · 5 November 2026

Two things have changed on Games by Zaib this week, and both are about making it easier to find your way around. Every game now has a proper About section, and this post doubles as a pocket tip sheet: one useful tip from each of our 46 games, picked from the guides we wrote by actually playing them.

An About section in every game

Until now, a game's title screen was mostly about getting you into the game. Now each of the 46 games has a small bar on its title screen with three buttons: Guide, About and Privacy. The bar only shows while the title screen is on top, and it looks for a spot that stays clear of the game's own buttons, so it should never get in the way of play.

Tap About and a full-screen page opens over the game. It has everything a new player needs in one place:

  • What the game is, in a couple of short paragraphs.
  • How to play, including the controls for phones and for a keyboard where the game has them.
  • Tips from Zaib, three short tips taken from the guide for that game.
  • Good to know, a short note about the game, such as how it saves your progress on your device.
  • A button to the full guide, which goes much deeper: first-run walkthroughs, tables, screenshots and a list of common questions.
  • Four similar games, in case you finish the one you came for.

Tap Back to the game (or press Esc on a computer) and you are straight back on the title screen. The About page is only a click away on the title screen and never opens by itself during play.

There are two reasons we made it. First, it saves you a trip: you can check the controls of a game before you start it, without leaving the page. Second, grown-ups can now see at a glance what a game is about and who made it. Every game is made by Zaib, and the About page says so.

Eight new games since mid-October

If you have not looked for a few weeks, there are new games to try. Burger Rush, Neon Nitro, Find Kedim!, Kedim Blocks and Noor Quest went live on 23 October. Mamak Mart and Pasar Malam Sort followed on 24 October, and Raya Style Studio on 25 October. Each one has its own guide and, as of today, its own About page on the title screen.

Who makes these games

The About page of the site now says it plainly: Games by Zaib is made by Jahan Zaib, an independent game maker based in Malaysia, building free games that play on any device. You can still reach Zaib from the contact page, and from now on new posts arrive twice a week.

One quick tip from every game

Here is the pocket sheet. Each tip comes from the guide linked beside it, where there is much more to read. They are grouped by kind of game, so jump to the section you like best.

Idle, business and farm games

  • Boss Town Tycoon: Hire a manager for each shop as soon as you can. A shop without a manager stops after every sale and earns nothing while you are away.
  • Chai Tycoon: Tap a lot in the first minute, then stop and buy. Buying is what makes you rich.
  • Corner Office: Tap quickly to build a COMBO, but play the tasks too. One quick minigame, even played badly, can do as much as a dozen taps.
  • Mamak Mart: Fill your hands at a counter, then serve several customers in one walk.
  • Burger Rush: Put the next patty on the grill while you stack the current burger.
  • Sunny Acres: Swipe, do not tap. Pick a seed once and swipe over every empty field.
  • Silly Sprout Garden: Water every sprout, more than once. Each splash cuts the grow time.
  • Dream Room: Check the must-haves first. Missing one caps you at 1 star, and 3 stars also need every must-have.
  • 30 Floors Up: Always fill the bathtub on Day 1. 40 litres of water is a huge head start.

Puzzle games

  • Juicy Merge: Never drop a big fruit into the middle of small ones. It buries them.
  • Block Glide: Look for the block that is in everyone's way, and move it first.
  • Kedim Blocks: Look at all three blocks before you drop the first one, and think about where the other two will go.
  • Kedim's Snack Swing: Watch a full swing or two before touching anything. Many levels show you the answer.
  • Number Dash: Blue gates (+ and ×) make your number bigger. Red gates (− and ÷) make it smaller.
  • Bus Bonanza: Read the queue first. Look a few colours ahead, not just at the front.
  • Rail Rush: Trains follow the glowing track, never the dark one, so check every switch.
  • Pasar Malam Sort: Look before you tap: count how many of each snack you can see, front and back, before your first move.
  • Find Kedim!: Zoom in close and sweep slowly across the picture, a strip at a time, like reading a page.

Pets, music and games for younger players

  • Baby Kedim: Start every visit with the daily gift, then fix the lowest need first.
  • Betta Bestie: Visit once a day rather than for a long time. A quick feed, a wipe of the glass and one mini-game keeps every meter healthy.
  • Beat Buddies: Tap a buddy on stage to Mute or Solo it, and hear how a song is built from layers.
  • Noor Quest: Say Bismillah before you start a round, just like Huda.
  • Raya Style Studio: Read all three wishes first: some are about Aisyah, some about Safwan, and some about both or the place.
  • Critter Keep Rescue: Stay out of the yellow vision cones and use bushes. You are hidden while standing or walking in a bush.
  • Kedim Therapist: Funny is never the best reply. The best answer is always Kind or Wise, so think about what the patient needs.

Action and adventure

  • Bolt Brigade: Duck straight away when you see a red ! above a bot, especially two at once.
  • Tank Rumble: Keep rolling in smooth circles. Stopping lets the robots' shots catch up with you.
  • Horde Breaker: Never stop moving. Walking in wide circles keeps the zombies behind you.
  • Fortress Frontline: Build your first archer near a bend in the road, so it shoots enemies for longer.
  • Meowstronauts: Drag from any empty spot. The rocket follows your movement, so your thumb never hides the action.
  • Skyfire Squadron: Stay near the bottom of the screen. It gives you the most time to see bullets coming.
  • Fin Frenzy: Swim through the middle of fish schools, not past them.
  • Gobble Hole: Start on the pavements. People, cones, bins and benches are quick to collect.
  • Noodle Clash: Stay near the middle at first, away from the deadly border and the crowded crash zones.
  • Pirate Isles: Drag anywhere and the ship sails towards your finger. Drag further to sail faster.
  • Kedimz Adventure: Stomp on enemies or paw swipe them, but jump over spiky ones or use a fire or ice shot.
  • Hop Heights: Look at the next platform, not your hopper, and aim toward where you want to land.
  • Neon Dash: Jump a little before the spike, not on it.
  • Sky Glide: Use short, steady taps rather than long holds.

Driving, flying and sports

  • Zaib City: Do the story first. It opens districts, pays well and introduces every job type.
  • Moto Mayhem: Stay on the road. Riding off-road caps your speed.
  • Neon Nitro: Press NITRO right as the lights say GO for a perfect start.
  • Haulway Heroes: Keep to the speed limit, stop at red lights and slow down for toll gates to avoid fines.
  • Horizon Pilot: To take off, go to full throttle, roll straight down the runway and pull back gently at take-off speed.
  • Goal Frenzy: Tap SHOOT for a placed shot, hold it for power, and swipe across it to curl the ball.
  • Shuttle Smash: Move the rival around: a clear to the back, then a drop to the front.

Where to go next

If you only try one thing this week, open a game you have not played yet, tap About on the title screen, read the three tips and then start. If a game catches your interest, its guide is one tap away from the same bar. You can also browse everything by kind on the categories page, or see every guide on the guides page.
