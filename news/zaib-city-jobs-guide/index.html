Zaib City Jobs: What All 11 Pay, Which Vehicle You Get, and How to Earn Three Stars

Zaib City has eleven jobs, from taxi fares to fire call-outs. We went through each one and wrote down what it pays, which vehicle you get, how the clock works and how to earn three stars, so you can pick the right job and earn more.

By Zaib · 9 November 2026

In Zaib City, jobs are how you earn most of your money between story missions. Coloured beams around the city mark where each job starts, and you can also open the phone, tap Jobs and start any job from the list. Each job card shows a line like "Pays ~$276+ · 90 XP", so you know roughly what you will earn.

Three rules that apply to every job

1. Reputation changes your pay. Every job has a normal price, but what you actually get depends on your Reputation. At 0 Reputation you get 80% of the normal pay, and at 100 you get 140%. You start the game with 20 Reputation, which means about 92%. That is why the Taxi Fares card shows about $276 at the start instead of the full $300. Each finished job adds 3 Reputation, and waiting at red lights helps too.

2. Leftover time is money. Most jobs have a clock. When you finish, many of them pay a bonus for every second still left on it. The Time Trial pays the most, $10 a second, and the Bus Route the least, $3 a second.

3. Stars depend on time left. For most timed jobs you get three stars if you finish with more than 30 seconds left, two stars with more than 8 seconds, and one star otherwise. The Time Trial only needs more than 15 seconds left, and the Parking Challenge more than 20. In the Street Race, stars come from your finishing place instead.

A job ends with no pay if the clock hits zero, if you stay out of the job vehicle for 30 seconds, or if you stop it yourself. Nothing else is lost, so just try again.

"Your own car" means the job uses whatever you are driving when you start it. If you start on foot, the Race, Time Trial and Stunt Showcase give you the Velo GT sports car, and the Parking Challenge gives you the little Zippy Hatch.

You never have to hunt for a taxi or a fire engine. If the right vehicle is parked close by, the game points you to it. If not, it puts one right next to you with a message like "Your City Cab is ready!"

The delivery jobs

Taxi Fares

Three passengers, one after another. A green beam shows where each one is waiting, and they wave when you pull up. Stop next to them, wait while they climb in, then follow the GPS line to their stop. You have 75 seconds to reach the first passenger, and the clock gets topped up for each trip. Every passenger pays their own fare on top of the job pay: longer trips pay more, and if you arrive with more than 20 seconds to spare, you also get a tip.

Tip: you must almost stop to pick up and drop off. Brake early so you do not roll past the beam.

Pizza Delivery

Three hot pizzas to three addresses in the suburb, midtown or downtown, on the Pizza Scooter. You have 70 seconds for the first one. Each delivery pays a tip, and the faster you are, the bigger the tip,

Parcel Run

Four parcels in the purple ZipBox Van, with 80 seconds for the first address. Every parcel adds $50 (changed by your Reputation) on top of the job pay.

Bus Route

The longest job and the best normal pay of the delivery-style jobs. You drive the City Bus to 8 bus stops with a 5-minute clock. At each stop, come to a full stop and wait about a second. Between one and five passengers hop on at every stop, and each one adds $12. Take turns wide: the bus is long.

The helper jobs

Ambulance Run

Two patients with sore ankles. Sirens switch on by themselves. You get 70 seconds to reach each patient and 60 seconds to bring them back to the hospital, and each patient adds $80.

Fire Response

Drive the Fire Engine to two fires and press SPRAY to hose them down. You have 150 seconds for both. Get close, and keep spraying until each fire is fully out.

Speed Patrol

A speeder is zooming through town, and your job is to give them three bumps in the Patrol Cruiser so they pull over. Nobody gets hurt, and the speeder says sorry at the end. You have 100 seconds. Light touches do not count, so line up and give a firm nudge, then a moment later go again. Stay close: if the speeder gets far away, they escape and the job ends.

The driving challenges

Street Race

You and three rivals race through the city streets with a checkpoint at every corner. Here is the nice part: second place still earns the full $900. Third place earns 60% and fourth earns 40%. Stars go 3 for first, 2 for second and 1 for anything else. There are three different race routes, and they take turns each time you race.

Tip: the race uses the car you are sitting in. Start it on foot to get the fast Velo GT, or drive over in a car you have upgraded. Do not start a race in the bus!

Time Trial

Hit every checkpoint before the clock runs out. There are three courses, and they come in order: Downtown Dash (85 seconds), Highway Loop (175 seconds) and Midtown Zigzag (70 seconds). With $10 for every second left, a fast run can pay a lot more than the normal $500. Finish with more than 15 seconds left for three stars.

Parking Challenge

Squeeze into a glowing bay between two parked cars, three times, each a little tighter. You have 70 seconds for the first bay and 50 seconds for each of the next two. To count, your car has to be lined up straight in the bay and stopped for a moment. Good news: you can face either way.

Tip: go slowly. Rolling in gently and stopping once is quicker than shuffling back and forth.

Stunt Showcase

Fly off three different ramps and stay in the air for more than one second each time. You have 4 minutes. Using the same ramp twice does not count (the game says "Try a different ramp!"). Sunset Hill has six named ramps close together, so this job is much easier once that area opens at Level 9 or after the Up the Hill story mission.

Extra money that is not a job

  • Daily challenges in the phone: three a day, worth $500 and 60 XP each. Many match jobs, such as "Deliver 4 pizzas" or "Put out 2 fires", so pick jobs that finish your dailies at the same time.
  • Hidden stars: each one pays $250 and 40 XP.
  • Stunt jumps: the first time you land each one, you get $300.
  • Trophies: every trophy pays $250, and several come from jobs, like Pizza Pro (10 pizzas), Firefighter (5 fires) and All Aboard (finish a bus route).
  • Level ups: each new level pays a bonus of $150 times the new level number.

Which job should you do first?

Start with Taxi Fares and Pizza Delivery. They are short, teach you to stop right on the beam, and raise your Reputation, so every later job pays more. Later, the Bus Route gives steady, calm money, and the Time Trial pays big for a short, fast drive. Save the Street Race for when you have a quick car.
